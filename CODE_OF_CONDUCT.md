# Code of conduct

Select Creators is written for creators of every size, niche and background. Everyone who takes part here, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else this project lives, agrees to keep it a place people want to come back to.

## Expected

- Be kind and specific. Critique the claim or the code, never the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you assume.
- Back up what you say. On this project, a source beats a strong opinion.
- Declare conflicts of interest when you have them.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, insults, or sexual or discriminatory language.
- Doxxing, or naming or describing private individuals.
- Posting links, handles or contact details that lead to gray-market sellers.
- Spam, self-promotion without disclosure, and undisclosed affiliate links.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report something privately, email support@creators.select. Reports are kept confidential.
