According to TechCrunch, LinkedIn added a **"seems like AI slop"** option that members can tap when a post looks AI-written. LinkedIn says it will use the signal to tune its models for detecting low-quality AI content.

LinkedIn also said it is:

- using new classifiers to reduce slop in suggested posts from outside your network,
- blocking "hundreds of thousands" of automated comment attempts every day,
- privately flagging content in members' dashboards when it seems inauthentic from heavy AI use, to help people improve their writing,
- pulling its "enhance your post" AI writer and replacing it with a proofreader that doesn't change your voice.

We couldn't read LinkedIn's own posts about this (LinkedIn blocks automated readers), so this is marked as reported. LinkedIn has not said how much a flag reduces a post's reach.
