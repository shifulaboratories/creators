Instagram is bringing **content protection**, a tool that until now was only on Facebook, to eligible Instagram creators. Once you're enrolled, every original video you post from then on is protected automatically, and you can add older videos by hand. The tool scans Instagram and Facebook for copies and shows each match with its view count and the follower count of the account that posted it.

For each match you pick one of four actions:

- **Track** (the default): leave it up and watch how it does.
- **Hide from everyone**: the copy can no longer be seen on Instagram or Facebook. Instagram says this reduces the copy's reach "without directly resulting in a penalty to the other profile."
- **Add credit link**: on Instagram, adds an "original by" label linking viewers to your video or profile.
- **Release the match**: drop your claim and let it stay up.

You can dispute ownership in the tool or file a copyright report through Instagram's Intellectual Property Reporting Center.

**The AI part.** Instagram says it is "starting to show videos in content protection that match the spoken words in your original video, even when the visuals have been replaced by AI." Testing starts with a small group of creators "in the coming weeks," and Instagram plans to expand it to creators enrolled on both Instagram and Facebook.
