Meta moved its creator tools from Meta Business Suite into **Creator Dashboard on web**: publishing and scheduling, the content library, Instagram insights and the inbox. The composer for Feed posts and Reels was rebuilt with a full-screen canvas and live preview, plus bulk video upload.

Meta says: "If you're eligible, you can now complete onboarding for stars, subscriptions and content monetization" on the web. Content protection is also there, to "identify potential unauthorized uses of your original content and take action," along with bulk invites for people who engage with you to follow your page.
