Meta added a **College Edition** to Creator Fast Track, the program it launched in March 2026 to pull creators from other platforms onto Facebook. It's for "college creators residing in the US with 1,000 or more followers on Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube" who apply "with a valid .edu email address."

Accepted creators get:

- "Increased reach on eligible reels"
- "Immediate access to Facebook Content Monetization"
- For creators with 20,000 or more followers, three months of guaranteed pay for sharing eligible Reels.

The guaranteed amounts Meta lists: **$100 to $450 a month** at 20,000 followers, **$1,000 a month** at 100,000, and **$3,000 a month** above one million. Creators can keep earning through Content Monetization after the program ends.
