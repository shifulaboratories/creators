Meta is rolling out **Your Algorithm**, AI tools that let people shape Facebook Feed and Reels by saying what they want. Meta says people can "directly add and manage the topics you want to see in Feed and Reels," and it lives in the three-dot menu on posts.

When someone taps **Interested** or **Not interested**, Meta "may ask follow up questions," and recommendations update in real time. Meta's post says the feature is rolling out and doesn't list countries or a completion date.
