YouTube calls these "the first significant changes since 2018" to the YouTube Partner Program (YPP), which it says has over 3 million creators.

**Joining YPP for ads and Premium revenue.** From February 1, 2027, "new creators applying for YPP will need 8,000 qualified watch hours in the last 365 days, or 20 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days." YouTube says: "This update won't impact creators already in YPP."

**Shorts revenue gets its own bar.** From the same date, "creators who have 10 million qualified Shorts views over the last 90 days will be eligible for ads and subscription revenue sharing on Shorts." Channels below that stay in YPP for long-form, and Shorts revenue sharing resumes automatically once they cross 10 million again. YouTube says creators who already earn significant revenue from Shorts are "unlikely to be impacted," and promises incentive programs for smaller Shorts channels: YouTube Shopping bonuses, brand-deal incentives and "earnings boosts for starting and growing trends," with details to come.

**Unchanged:** "The entry thresholds for our Fan Funding and shopping products remain unchanged."

**Premium Lite** expands to every country with YouTube Premium. YouTube puts 30% of net Premium subscription revenue and 60% of Premium Lite revenue into the creator pool, split 55% to long-form and 45% to Shorts.
