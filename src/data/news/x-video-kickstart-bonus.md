On November 25, 2026, X announced new creation tools, as TechCrunch reported. The upgraded media editor lets creators record in segments, convert horizontal video to vertical, "automatically generate captions, trim footage, add text overlays, choose custom thumbnails, and adjust photos before publishing." Availability "varies by region, X said."

X's help center describes the **Video Kickstart Bonus** as "an invite-only pilot program that pays creators a bonus for posting their own short-form, vertical videos." To earn it: "Post 15 qualifying videos on 15 different days during a program period." Periods last "about one month." A qualifying video is "Vertical: a 9:16 aspect ratio," "Under 3 minutes long," and your own. Several videos on one day count as one day.

"Your bonus amount is shown in your invitation and in Creator Studio," and it's paid in your next Original Content Rewards payout. It's separate from those rewards and doesn't change them. Invited creators are also enrolled in Original Content Rewards automatically, and get Premium+ free for one year if they don't have it. X "may change or end the program at any time."

This follows X's clipping feature earlier in November: clips credit and link back to the original, and per X, "Both the original creator and the clipper can earn from views on the clip through Original Content Rewards."
