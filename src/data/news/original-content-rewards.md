X closed **Creator Revenue Sharing** to new members on August 7, 2026, and opened applications for **Original Content Rewards** on September 8, 2026. Members of the old program had to reapply.

To qualify, X's help center lists: 18 or older, an eligible country, good standing, an active Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business subscription, at least **500 verified followers**, and at least **500,000 Home timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days**. Replies don't count toward that number.

Payouts are based on "qualified impressions": unique views from Premium subscribers on the Home timeline where at least half the post is on screen. Repeat views, promoted views and fake views don't count. The minimum payout is $30, paid every two weeks. X doesn't publish a per-impression rate.
