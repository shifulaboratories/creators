## How this list works

- **Every line has a source.** **Official** means at least one link is the platform itself: its blog, newsroom, help center or published code. **Reported** means we're relying on credible outlets like TechCrunch, Tubefilter or Social Media Today.
- **Dated by announcement.** If a change took effect later, the card says when. If no source gives the exact day, we show the month.
- **Big ones** changed how a platform ranks or pays most creators.
- **New changes land in [News](/news/) first**, with what they mean for you, and show up here the same day.
- Spotted a mistake or a missing change? See [Corrections](/about/corrections/). Get new ones in any feed reader with the [RSS feed](/news/feed.xml).
