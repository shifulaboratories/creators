**The short version:** most full-time creators don’t live on one paycheck. They stack a few: platform payouts for the base, brand deals for the big months, and something they own (a membership, a product) so a single algorithm change can’t wipe them out. This section shows what each one actually pays, using the platforms’ own fee pages and published rate data, never “I made $10K in a week” screenshots.

## Start here

1. **See every option side by side.** [Monetization paths](/earn/monetization-paths/) compares payouts, brand deals, memberships, products and affiliate links on what you keep, how steady it is, and who owns your fans.
2. **Know your number before a brand asks.** [Pricing your rates](/earn/pricing/) puts every published rate benchmark on one chart for your platform and size.
3. **Learn the deal from start to finish.** [Brand deals 101](/earn/brand-deals/) walks through brief, quote, contract, delivery and invoice.
4. **Build something you own.** [Paywalls and memberships](/earn/paywalls-and-memberships/) compares what you take home on YouTube, Patreon, Substack, Ghost and beehiiv.

When a contract shows up, jump to [Deals and contracts](/deals/).
