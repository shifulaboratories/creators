**The short version:** an affiliate link pays you a commission when someone buys through it. It’s the easiest income to start (you can do it today, with no minimum audience) and it compounds on content people find through search: reviews, tutorials, “what I use” videos. The rules: only link what you’d recommend anyway, disclose every time, and read the program’s terms before you build content around it.

## Where it pays off

<span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>

- **Search content beats feed content.** A “best camera for beginners” video earns for years. A trending Reel earns for days.
- **Things people were going to buy anyway.** Gear, software, beauty, books, kitchen, travel.
- **Recurring products.** Software and subscriptions that pay a commission every month the customer stays are worth far more than one-off sales.
- **Your own storefront.** A single link page with everything you use keeps links findable after the video.

## How to judge a program

Before you build content around a program, read its terms and look for: <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>

| Look at | Good sign | Watch out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Commission** | Clear rate per product category, in writing | “Up to” rates that apply to almost nothing |
| **Cookie window** | Long enough for how people buy that product | A day or less on something people think about |
| **Payout** | Low minimum, regular schedule | High minimum, long holds, payouts in store credit |
| **Rules** | Clear on where you can post links | Bans on links in email or ads you didn’t read about |
| **Changes** | Notice before rate cuts | Rates cut overnight on content you already made |

Commission rates change often, so we don’t list them here. Check each program’s own rate page, not a blog post that copied it a year ago.

## Disclose it, every time

The FTC is explicit that a commission counts as a financial relationship you have to disclose, even if you’d recommend the product anyway: <span class="sc-tag is-platform">From the law</span>

- Put the disclosure **next to the link or the recommendation**, not only on your profile or at the bottom of a description.
- In videos, **say it out loud** as well as in the caption, because a caption-only disclosure is easy to miss.
- Use plain words: “I earn a commission if you buy through my links.” Avoid abbreviations like “sp” or “spon,” which the FTC specifically says not to use.

More in [Disclosing sponsored posts](/deals/disclosure/).

## Our own affiliate rules

This site follows the same rules it teaches: every affiliate link is labeled where it appears, every recommendation names a free or non-affiliate alternative, and rankings don’t depend on commissions. See [How we make money](/about/how-we-make-money/).
