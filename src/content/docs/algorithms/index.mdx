## How we rank evidence

<figure class="sc-figure">
<ol class="sc-steps">
<li class="is-active"><strong>Published code</strong><span>What the system actually computes. X is the only major platform that publishes its feed's ranking code.</span></li>
<li><strong>The platform's own explainers</strong><span>Help centers, engineering blogs, newsroom posts. Accurate, but high level.</span></li>
<li><strong>Platform employees on the record</strong><span>Engineers and product leads answering creators' questions in interviews.</span></li>
<li><strong>Practitioners with a track record</strong><span>Strategists and creators speaking under their own name about what they've seen work.</span></li>
<li><strong>Everyone else</strong><span>Anonymous tips, "I tested this" threads, screenshot charts. We don't cite these.</span></li>
</ol>
<figcaption>Every claim on this site traces to one of the first four. If it can't, we say so or leave it out.</figcaption>
</figure>
