**The short version:** hits are rare, and most of the people a hit reaches never see you again. The research on a billion shares put true viral hits at around one in a million. So the creators who win don't bet on one post. They make each hit a doorway: a reason to follow, a next post waiting, and a body of work people are glad they found.

## Give people a reason to come back

Feeds reward this directly. YouTube counts "valued watchtime" from viewers who rate a video four or five stars in surveys, and Facebook weights its predictions by what people say is "worth their time." In X's published code, a predicted follow is weighted 4, against 0.708 for a like. A post people regret watching hurts you even if they finished it.

## Turn the hit into a series

The fastest way to keep new viewers is to give them more of exactly what they came for. Name it, number it, and make it recognizable from the first frame. [Formats that travel](/grow/formats/) shows how to turn your best post into a repeatable series.

## See the bigger picture

Going viral is the top of the funnel, not the business. People rarely follow, subscribe or buy after seeing you once; it usually takes many impressions across many posts. That's why creators also use clips, fan pages and multiple platforms to stay in front of the same people. Our [Strategy guides](/strategy/) cover that side: [why it takes multiple impressions](/strategy/multiple-impressions/), [clipping](/strategy/clipping/) and [fan pages](/strategy/fan-pages/).
