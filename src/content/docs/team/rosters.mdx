**The short version:** agencies rarely publish their client lists, but the trade press reports signings. We collected those reports so you can see who an agency works with before you sign. Each one is a dated news report, not a live roster, so treat it as a starting point for your own reference calls.

**What it tells you:** agencies run on everyday tools (Google Workspace, Slack, project boards like Asana and Airtable) plus creator-data platforms like CreatorIQ and Traackr. Nothing exotic, which means a well-organized solo creator can run on the same stack.

<DeepDive title="How this list is made" hint="Sources, rules, and how to fix or remove a listing">

### Where the data comes from

- **Signings:** published reporting, mostly [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/), a trade publication that covers the creator industry. We read headlines, teasers and articles and record only what they say.
- **"Named as a client":** the article lists the creator as on the company's roster, without reporting a new signing.
- **"Left":** a published report that the relationship ended.
- **Software:** the agency's own public job postings. Postings expire, so each one shows the date we read it.

### What we don't do

- **No LinkedIn, no scraping behind logins, nothing against a site's terms.** We read public pages the way any reader would.
- **No staff names.** We list companies, not the people who work there.
- **No minors.** We leave out kids and family channels built around children, even when they were reported.
- **No guessing.** If a report doesn't name a creator, neither do we.

### Fix or remove a listing

If you're named here and something is wrong or out of date, or you'd rather not be listed, email [support@creators.select](mailto:support@creators.select). We'll correct or remove it. To add a reported signing, see [Contribute](/about/contribute/) and include the article link.
