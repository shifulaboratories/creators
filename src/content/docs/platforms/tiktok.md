**The short version:** TikTok ranks each video for each viewer based mostly on how people interact with it. Follower count and past hits are not direct factors, so every video gets a fresh chance. The money, though, now goes to videos over a minute long.

## What TikTok says it uses

From the platform:

- **User interactions:** videos you like, share, comment on, the accounts you follow, and the content you create.
- **Video information:** captions, sounds and hashtags.
- **Device and account settings:** language, country and device type. These get lower weight.
- **Stronger signals count more.** TikTok's example: whether someone finishes watching a longer video from beginning to end.
- **Not direct factors:** follower count, and whether the account had high-performing videos before.
- **Variety rules:** the feed generally won't show two videos in a row with the same sound or by the same creator, and won't recommend duplicates or videos someone has already seen.
- **Not eligible:** videos still under review, spam, and content trying to artificially boost traffic.

## What can keep you off For You

In 2024, TikTok added "eligibility standards" to its Community Guidelines. A video can be allowed on TikTok and still not be recommended.

## What to optimize for

- **Completion.** Since finishing a longer video is a strong signal, cut anything that doesn't earn the next second. *(From the platform, applied.)*
- **The first seconds.** Viewers decide fast in a swipe feed, so open on the payoff or the question. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Shares and comments over likes.** Make something people want to send to a friend or argue about. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Describe the video clearly.** Captions, sounds and hashtags are how TikTok understands what it is. *(From the platform.)*
- **Make things people search for.** "Search value" is one of the four things the Creator Rewards Program scores, and TikTok gives creators a Creator Search Insights tool to find what people are looking for. *(From the platform.)*
- **Don't buy engagement.** Content that tries to artificially increase traffic is ineligible for For You, and buying fake engagement is also a legal risk in the US. See [Fake engagement](/underground/fake-engagement/).

## Formats that work

- **Videos over a minute.** TikTok said in March 2024 that people spend 50% of their time on TikTok watching videos longer than a minute, and that's what its rewards program pays for. *(From the platform.)*
- **Short, punchy videos** still win reach. Use them to get found, and longer ones to get paid. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **LIVE**, where gifts and subscriptions come in real time. *(From the platform.)*
- **Series built on search.** A run of videos answering the questions your niche types into the search bar. *(Creator-tested.)*

- **Creator Rewards Program.** Pays for original videos over a minute, scored on four things: originality, play duration (watch time and finish rate), search value, and audience engagement (likes, comments and shares). *(From the platform.)*
- **Subscriptions.** Fans pay monthly for perks like subscriber-only videos, LIVEs and badges, in up to three tiers. Same bar as above (10K followers and 100K views in the past month), or LIVE-specific requirements. *(From the platform, Sept 2024.)*
- **LIVE gifts.** Viewers send virtual gifts during LIVEs, which turn into earnings. *(From the platform.)*
- **TikTok Shop affiliate and brand deals** are a big share of creator income too. We haven't sourced their current terms yet, so check TikTok's Creator Center. *(Creator-tested.)*
