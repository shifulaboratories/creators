**The short version:** YouTube recommends videos people click, keep watching, and later say they were glad they watched. Recommendations drive more viewing than subscriptions or search, so this is the system to design for. It's also the platform with the clearest path to getting paid.

## What YouTube says it uses

From the platform:

- **Where recommendations appear:** the Homepage and the Up Next panel. Recommendations drive "a significant amount of the overall viewership," more than subscriptions or search.
- **Signals:** clicks, watch time, survey responses, shares, likes and dislikes. YouTube added watch time in 2012 because clicks alone rewarded misleading videos.
- **Valued watch time:** YouTube surveys viewers and counts time on videos they rate highly (4 or 5 stars) as more valuable.
- **No fixed formula:** how much each signal counts depends on the viewer.
- **Not your social graph:** YouTube says its recommendations don't rely on who you know, unlike social networks.
- **Search** ranks on relevance, engagement and quality.

- **Clicks without satisfaction backfire.** Since YouTube weighs watch time and surveys, a misleading thumbnail wins the click and loses the recommendation. *(From the platform, applied.)*
- **Think in viewers, not subscribers.** Because recommendations don't depend on your social graph, a new channel can reach people who have never heard of it. *(From the platform, applied.)*
- **Read retention graphs.** Where viewers drop is where the video stopped earning their time. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Use the [MrBeast production handbook takeaways](/shortlist/mrbeast-production-handbook/)** for a detailed take on packaging and retention from the largest channel on the platform.

## How you get paid

The YouTube Partner Program has two doors. The first unlocks fan money and shopping; the second adds a share of ad revenue.

<div class="sc-stat is-jade"><span class="sc-stat__value">1,000</span><span class="sc-stat__label">subscribers, plus 4,000 watch hours in 12 months or 10M Shorts views in 90 days</span></div>
</div>
<figcaption>Expanded tier (left) unlocks memberships, Supers, gifts and Shopping. Full tier (right) adds ad revenue and YouTube Premium revenue. Expanded tier only in listed countries, including the US, UK, Canada, Australia and India.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Fan funding:** channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks, and Jewels and gifts. *(From the platform.)*
- **Shopping:** tag products in your videos once you accept the Commerce Product Module. *(From the platform.)*
- **Ads and Premium:** a share of ad revenue on long videos and the Shorts feed, plus a share of what Premium members pay to watch you. *(From the platform.)*
- **Stay active.** YouTube may turn off monetization for channels with no uploads or Posts activity for six months or more. *(From the platform.)*
- **Heads up:** YouTube is updating Partner Program terms on February 1, 2027. You'll need to accept them in YouTube Studio by January 31, 2027. *(From the platform.)*
- **Brand deals usually beat ads.** Most full-time YouTubers earn more from sponsorships than from AdSense. See [Brand deals](/earn/brand-deals/). *(Creator-tested.)*
