**The short version:** Every other guide here is about renting attention from an algorithm. A newsletter is the audience you own: if a platform bans you or changes the rules, your list still works. The newer twist is that newsletter platforms now have their own discovery networks, so you don't have to grow from zero.

## Grow with the networks, not from zero

- **Platform networks.** Substack says its network drives 25% of paid subscriptions on the platform, and more than 25% of subscriptions start in its apps.
- **Recommendations.** On Substack, writers recommend each other's publications, and Notes works like a social feed inside the app.
- **Your social accounts.** Most writers still grow by pointing their social audience to the sign-up page.

## How you get paid: Substack vs beehiiv

<figure class="sc-figure">
<div class="sc-compare">
<div class="sc-compare__side is-champagne"><h4>Substack</h4><ul><li>Free to start</li><li>Takes 10% of paid subscription revenue</li><li>Plus Stripe: 2.9% + 30¢ per card payment, 0.7% on recurring billing</li><li>Built-in network, Notes and app</li><li>More than 5 million paid subscriptions (March 2025)</li></ul></div>
<div class="sc-compare__side is-jade"><h4>beehiiv</h4><ul><li>Paid plans from $49/month (Lite) or $95/month (Pro), billed yearly</li><li>Takes 0% of paid subscriptions on those plans</li><li>Plus Stripe: 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction</li><li>Ad Network pays per open (CPM) or click (CPC)</li></ul></div>
</div>
<figcaption>From each platform's pricing and help pages, checked Nov 26, 2026</figcaption>
</figure>

**A quick way to choose:** a flat fee beats a percentage once you're earning enough. At beehiiv's $95 Pro plan, Substack's 10% costs more once paid revenue passes about $950 a month. Below that, Substack's free start and network are hard to beat.

Other ways newsletters earn:

- **Sponsorships.** One sponsor slot per issue, priced per thousand opens.
- **Ad networks** like beehiiv's, which match you with advertisers automatically.
- **Products.** Courses, templates and guides sold to the people who already read you.

See also: [Paywalls and memberships](/earn/paywalls-and-memberships/).
