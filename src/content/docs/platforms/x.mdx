**The short version:** X picks a pool of candidate posts from people you follow and people you don't, ranks them by how likely you are to engage, then filters for variety and safety. X has published its code twice, in 2023 and in January 2026. Since September 2026, X pays creators for original posts seen by Premium subscribers.

## Half the feed is strangers

In 2023, X said about half of each For You feed came from accounts people didn't follow. So a strong post can travel well past your followers. Every feed goes through three steps:

## Write for replies and shares

X has published the weights it gives each action. In the 2026 code, sharing via copy link counts most, and replies, quotes and DM shares each outweigh a like several times over. See every weight in [Inside X's open-source algorithm](/algorithms/x-open-source/).

The 2026 system learns from behavior rather than fixed rules, so treat any specific “hack” as temporary. The direction is stable: posts people talk about and pass on.

Threads and Articles are for depth; a single sharp post is for reach. Video, images and text count the same for Original Content Rewards, though X also runs an invite-only bonus for vertical video (below). Commentary counts as original if you add a genuine point of view.

## How you get paid

X closed Creator Revenue Sharing to new members on August 7, 2026, and replaced it with **Original Content Rewards**, open since September 8, 2026.

You're paid for unique views from **Premium subscribers** on the Home timeline, so a post that only travels among free accounts earns little even if it goes wide. Repeat, promoted and fake views don't count, and X doesn't publish a rate. Bots, automation and repeated engagement requests get you removed.

On top of that, X runs an invite-only **Video Kickstart Bonus** (announced Nov 25, 2026): invited creators earn a bonus for posting their own 9:16 videos under 3 minutes on 15 different days in a period of about a month, paid with their next Original Content Rewards payout. See [X Help Center](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/video-kickstart-bonus).

<DeepDive title="What X has said, in its own words" hint="The 2023 and 2026 code releases">

### 2023 release (X Engineering)

- **Candidate sourcing:** about 1,500 candidate posts per request, on average half from accounts you follow and half from accounts you don't.
- **Ranking:** a neural network of about 48 million parameters predicts the probability of several kinds of engagement. Likes, reposts and replies are named as positive engagement.
- **Filtering:** heuristics and filters build "a balanced and diverse feed."

### January 2026 release (as reported by TechCrunch)

- X released new code for the feed. It relies on a **Grok-based transformer** that learns relevance from sequences of what users engage with, with no hand-built features for content relevance.
- Filters remove blocked accounts, muted keywords, and content judged too violent or spam-like. Ranking weighs the likelihood of a like, reply, repost or other engagement, plus diversity.

### What counts for payouts

"Qualified impressions": unique views from Premium subscribers on the Home timeline where at least half the post is on screen.
