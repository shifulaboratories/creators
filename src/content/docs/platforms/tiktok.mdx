**The short version:** TikTok ranks each video for each viewer based mostly on how people interact with it. Follower count and past hits are not direct factors, so every video gets a fresh chance. The money, though, now goes to videos over a minute long.

## Every video starts from zero

TikTok says follower count and your past hits are not direct factors. Each video is judged on how people react to *it*. That's good news if you're small, and it means one flop doesn't sink the next video.

## Get them to the end

Finishing a longer video is the example TikTok gives of a strong signal. So the edit matters more than the idea: open on the payoff, then make every cut earn the next second.

## Stay eligible for For You

A video can be allowed on TikTok and still never be recommended. Since 2024, TikTok's guidelines spell out what isn't eligible. Accounts that keep posting it can lose For You as a whole and get harder to find in search.

## Make videos people search for

TikTok is a search engine for a lot of people. Search value is one of the four things Creator Rewards scores, and TikTok gives you **Creator Search Insights** to see what people are typing. A run of videos answering your niche's top questions keeps getting found long after For You moves on.

Describe each video plainly in the caption. Captions, sounds and hashtags are how TikTok understands what a video is.

Use short videos to get found and longer ones to get paid. TikTok says people spend half their time on TikTok watching videos over a minute, and that's what it rewards.

| Program | How it pays |
| --- | --- |
| **Creator Rewards** | Original videos over a minute, scored on originality, play duration, search value and engagement |
| **Subscriptions** | Fans pay monthly for subscriber-only videos, LIVEs and badges, up to three tiers. Same 10K / 100K bar |
| **LIVE gifts** | Viewers send virtual gifts during LIVEs that turn into earnings |
| **Shop and brand deals** | A big share of creator income. We haven't sourced current Shop terms yet: check Creator Center |

For brand money, start with [Brand deals 101](/deals/brand-deals/).

### What TikTok says it uses

- **User interactions:** videos you like, share, comment on, the accounts you follow, and the content you create.
- **Video information:** captions, sounds and hashtags.
- **Device and account settings:** language, country and device type. These get lower weight.
- **Stronger signals count more.** TikTok's example: whether someone finishes watching a longer video from beginning to end.
- **Not direct factors:** follower count, and whether the account had high-performing videos before.
- **Variety rules:** the feed generally won't show two videos in a row with the same sound or by the same creator, and won't recommend duplicates or videos someone has already seen.
- **Not eligible:** videos still under review, spam, and content trying to artificially boost traffic.

### Eligibility standards (2024)

Allowed but not recommended: sexually suggestive content, dangerous activities and challenges, many kinds of weight-loss and dieting content, unverified claims about an unfolding emergency, and videos from accounts under 16. As reported by Engadget and Tubefilter, April 2024.

Buying fake engagement is also a legal risk in the US. See [Fake engagement](/underground/fake-engagement/).
