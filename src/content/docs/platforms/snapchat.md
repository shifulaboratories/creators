**The short version:** Snapchat recommends public content in three places: Spotlight, Public Stories (including Discover), and the Snap Map. Snap says plainly that "allowed on Snapchat doesn't always mean recommended." Its creator payout program rewards one thing above all: posting a lot, every week.

## What Snap says it uses

From the platform:

- **Technology plus people.** Snap moderates recommended content "with a blend of technology and human review."
- **Whole-account view.** Snap judges accounts, not only single posts. Repeated rule-breaking can reduce an account's reach, for example by not recommending it to teens, new users, or some countries.
- **Signals for limiting reach:** moderation history, user feedback, engagement signals, and Snap's own editorial judgment.
- **Human-made content is favored.** Snap's ranking "rewards authentic, human-made content over wholly AI-generated content created outside of Snapchat," even when it's labeled. AI made with Snapchat's own tools is fine.
- **Not eligible for recommendation:** reposting content you didn't make without real transformation, low-effort "reactions" that are just a reason to repost, the same Snap posted over and over, links or QR codes to other social apps, and promoting your accounts on other platforms.

## What to optimize for

- **Make it here, make it yours.** Original, human-made Snaps are what the ranking rewards. *(From the platform.)*
- **Keep other apps' handles off your Snaps.** Promoting other platforms makes a Snap ineligible for recommendation. Links on your own profile are fine but won't be promoted. *(From the platform.)*
- **Watch your sensitive-content mix.** Snap limits how much sensitive content it recommends and may not show it to minors or new users. *(From the platform.)*
- **Post daily.** The payout program literally counts posting days, and Stories reward habit. *(From the platform, applied.)*

## Formats that work

- **Spotlight videos over a minute.** Those are the ones that can carry ads and earn. *(From the platform.)*
- **Daily Public Stories.** Your subscribers' habit, and the other half of the payout program. *(From the platform.)*
- **Location-tagged moments** for the Snap Map: events, food, travel. *(Creator-tested.)*

## How you get paid

In February 2025, Snap replaced its Spotlight Rewards fund with one **unified monetization program** that places ads in your Stories and in Spotlight videos longer than a minute.
