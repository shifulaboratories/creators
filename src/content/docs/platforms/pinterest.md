**The short version:** People come to Pinterest to plan something: a kitchen, a wedding, a weeknight dinner. They search, save, and click through to buy or learn. Your Pins don't expire after a day, so Pinterest is the slow, compounding platform, and it sends traffic off the app better than almost any other.

## What Pinterest says it uses

From the platform:

- **Personal, not chronological.** The home feed is built around what each person is planning, not when you posted.
- **Pins travel beyond your followers,** into home feeds and search results.
- **Ranking uses** "strong keywords, original images and link quality" (June 2025).
- **Fresh ideas win.** Pinterest says it highlights "original ideas and imagery that are new to the platform."
- **Engagement grows reach:** saves, clicks, comments and shares.
- **Pins resurface for years.** "Standout Pins can keep surfacing over time, even as interests and seasons change."

## What to optimize for

- **Write titles like search queries.** Pinterest asks for "a descriptive title" with "clear details and strong keywords" in the description. Think "easy 20-minute pasta for two," not "Tuesday vibes." *(From the platform.)*
- **Post new images, not repins.** Originality and newness are named signals. *(From the platform.)*
- **Make the link worth it.** Link quality is a ranking signal, so send people to a page that delivers what the Pin shows. *(From the platform.)*
- **Plan seasons early.** People plan ahead, so holiday and wedding content should go up well before the date. Pinterest's free [Trends tool](https://trends.pinterest.com/) shows when searches start rising. *(Creator-tested, using Pinterest's tool.)*
- **Keep boards alive.** Pinterest suggests refreshing boards with new Pins regularly. *(From the platform.)*

## How you get paid

- **Traffic is the product.** Most Pinterest money is earned off Pinterest: on your blog, shop, newsletter or storefront. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Affiliate links** are allowed on Pins, and creators can tag products. *(From the platform, 2021 announcement.)*
- **Paid partnerships.** A "paid partnership" label for sponsored Pins, which the brand approves. It launched for select creators in 18 countries in 2021, so check what's available to you now. *(From the platform.)*
- **Brand deals for home, food, fashion and wedding creators**, where Pinterest is often the brand's best-converting channel. *(Creator-tested.)*
