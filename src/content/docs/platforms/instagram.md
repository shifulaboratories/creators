**The short version:** Instagram has no single algorithm. Feed, Explore, Reels, Stories and Search each rank differently, and Reels and Explore are mostly content from accounts people don't follow yet. That's where new reach comes from.

## What Instagram says it uses

From the platform:

- **Feed** signals, roughly in order of importance: your activity (likes, shares, saves, comments), information about the post (including how quickly people engage), information about the poster, and your history with them.
- **Explore** predicts likes, saves and shares, and popularity "matter[s] much more" than in Feed or Stories.
- **Reels** predicts reshares, watching all the way through, likes, and visits to the audio page.
- **What limits a Reel's reach:** low resolution or watermarks from other apps, muted audio or borders, mostly-text Reels, and reposts of other people's content.
- **Recommendation guidelines** apply to Explore, Reels, Search and suggested posts. Accounts that repeatedly break them can lose eligibility for a while. **Account Status** in the app shows whether you're eligible.
- **No pay-to-play suppression:** Instagram says it doesn't suppress content to encourage people to buy ads.

## What to optimize for

- **Make Reels people reshare and finish.** Those are the first two actions Instagram names for Reels. *(From the platform.)*
- **Post original, native files.** Export clean, high-resolution video without another app's watermark. *(From the platform.)*
- **Use Feed for your existing audience** and Reels to reach new people. *(From the platform, applied.)*
- **Check Account Status before assuming a "shadowban."** It shows recommendation eligibility directly. *(From the platform.)*
- **Saves and shares beat likes** for content meant to be useful. *(Creator-tested.)*
