**The short version:** Twitch is where people hang out with you live. Unlike TikTok or YouTube, Twitch hasn't published an explainer of how it recommends channels, so most growth comes from bringing people in from other platforms. The money is the most direct of any platform: fans pay you, in real time.

## What we know about discovery

- **No published ranking explainer.** We haven't found a Twitch document describing how it recommends streams, the way YouTube, TikTok and Instagram have. Treat any "Twitch algorithm hack" with suspicion. *(Not stated by the platform.)*
- **Raids, hosts and reruns don't count** toward Partner viewership, so Twitch measures people who chose to watch you. *(From the platform.)*
- **Short clips are the front door.** Streamers grow by cutting highlights for short-video apps, which do have recommendation feeds. *(Creator-tested.)*

## What to optimize for

- **Consistency.** A fixed schedule turns one-time viewers into regulars, and the Partner path is measured in streams and days. *(Creator-tested, consistent with Twitch's criteria.)*
- **Average viewers, not peak.** Every Twitch milestone counts your average concurrent viewers. *(From the platform.)*
- **Chat is the content.** Read names, run polls and Channel Points rewards, give people a reason to talk. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Clip everything.** Your best five minutes, posted to Shorts, TikTok and Reels, are how strangers find the stream. *(Creator-tested.)*

## How you get paid

Twitch made big changes in May 2026: subs, Bits, emotes, badges and Channel Points opened up to streamers who aren't Affiliates yet. You still need Affiliate or Partner status to cash out.

- **Subscriptions:** $5.99, $9.99 or $24.99 a month, or a free Prime Gaming sub. *(From the platform.)*
- **Bits:** viewers Cheer with Bits, and Partners earn 1 cent per Bit. *(From the platform.)*
- **Ads:** a share of ad revenue on your channel, and you control mid-roll length and frequency. *(From the platform.)*
- **Plus Program:** a bigger sub split for consistent streamers, open to Affiliates and Partners. 60/40 if you hold 100 Plus Points for three months in a row, 70/30 at 300 points. A Tier 1 sub is 1 point, Tier 2 is 2, Tier 3 is 6. *(Reporting, TechCrunch, Jan 2024.)*
- **Sponsorships** are where established streamers earn most. See [Brand deals](/deals/brand-deals/). *(Creator-tested.)*

## The path to Partner

Partner adds perks like longer VOD storage (60 days versus 14), up to 50 emotes and a verified badge. You qualify through the **Path to Partner** achievement in your dashboard, or by showing a large, engaged audience elsewhere. Twitch says completing the achievement "does not guarantee Partnership." *(From the platform.)*
