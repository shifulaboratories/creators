**The short version:** YouTube recommends videos people click, keep watching, and later say they were glad they watched. Recommendations drive more viewing than subscriptions or search, so this is the system to design for. It's also the platform with the clearest path to getting paid.

## Recommendations are the whole game

YouTube says Home and Up Next drive more viewing than subscriptions or search, and they don't depend on who already knows you. A brand-new channel can land in front of millions. To get there, a video has to win four moments in a row.

## Make a promise, then keep it

YouTube added watch time in 2012 because clicks alone rewarded misleading videos. It now also surveys viewers and counts time on videos they rate highly as worth more. So the thumbnail's job is to make a promise the video keeps in the first seconds.

- **Keep posting.** YouTube may turn off monetization after six months with no uploads or Posts.
- **Accept the new terms.** Partner Program terms change on February 1, 2027. Accept them in YouTube Studio by January 31, 2027.
- **Brand deals usually beat ads.** Most full-time YouTubers earn more from sponsorships than AdSense. Start with [Brand deals 101](/deals/brand-deals/).

<DeepDive title="What YouTube has said, in its own words" hint="Signals, valued watch time, the full list of ways to earn, who to follow">

### What YouTube says it uses

- **Where recommendations appear:** the Homepage and the Up Next panel. Recommendations drive "a significant amount of the overall viewership," more than subscriptions or search.
- **Signals:** clicks, watch time, survey responses, shares, likes and dislikes. YouTube added watch time in 2012 because clicks alone rewarded misleading videos.
- **Valued watch time:** YouTube surveys viewers and counts time on videos they rate highly (4 or 5 stars) as more valuable.
- **No fixed formula:** how much each signal counts depends on the viewer.
- **Not your social graph:** YouTube says its recommendations don't rely on who you know, unlike social networks.
- **Search** ranks on relevance, engagement and quality.

### Every way YouTube pays

- **Fan funding:** channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks, and Jewels and gifts.
- **Shopping:** tag products in your videos once you accept the Commerce Product Module.
- **Ads and Premium:** a share of ad revenue on long videos and the Shorts feed, plus a share of what Premium members pay to watch you.
- **Shorts route in:** 10M Shorts views in 90 days qualifies for the full tier. Over 400M hours a month of podcasts were watched on TVs in 2024.

### Who to follow for changes

- **[Creator Insider](https://www.youtube.com/@CreatorInsider)**: a channel run by YouTube's own teams, explaining features and tests before they ship widely.
- **Rene Ritchie, YouTube's Creator Liaison**, answers algorithm questions on the record.
