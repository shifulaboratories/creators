**The short version:** Instagram has no single algorithm. Feed, Explore, Reels, Stories and Search each rank differently, and Reels and Explore are mostly content from accounts people don't follow yet. That's where new reach comes from.

## Two feeds, two jobs

Your followers see you in Feed and Stories. Everyone else finds you in Reels and Explore. Make each post for one of those jobs, not both.

## Make Reels people send to a friend

Instagram's head has named sends as a top signal in every statement we cite from 2024 and 2025. A post gets sent when it's obviously *for someone*: a specific friend, a group chat, a partner. Useful to everyone usually means sent to no one.

**Trial Reels** are the closest thing to a free test: Instagram shows the Reel only to people who don't follow you, then shows you how it did. Use them to try a new idea without it landing in your followers' feeds.

## How you get paid

Instagram doesn't publish one follower threshold. Each tool shows whether you qualify in your Professional Dashboard.

| From | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Brands** | Brand deals (still the biggest for most creators), Partnership Ads, Creator Marketplace |
| **Fans** | Subscriptions at a monthly price you set, Gifts on Reels, Badges in Lives |
| **Instagram** | Invite-only bonuses for Reels, carousels and photos that perform |

Subscription payouts land on the 21st of the following month. For brand money, start with [Brand deals 101](/deals/brand-deals/) and the [rate check](/deals/pricing/).

### What Instagram says it uses

- **Feed** signals, roughly in order of importance: your activity (likes, shares, saves, comments), information about the post (including how quickly people engage), information about the poster, and your history with them.
- **Explore** predicts likes, saves and shares, and popularity “matter[s] much more” than in Feed or Stories.
- **Reels** predicts reshares, watching all the way through, likes, and visits to the audio page.
- **What limits a Reel's reach:** low resolution or watermarks from other apps, muted audio or borders, mostly-text Reels, and reposts of other people's content.
- **Recommendation guidelines** apply to Explore, Reels, Search and suggested posts. Accounts that repeatedly break them can lose eligibility for a while. **Account Status** shows whether you're eligible.
- **No pay-to-play suppression:** Instagram says it doesn't hold content back to push people to buy ads.

### In Mosseri's words

Adam Mosseri, head of Instagram, answers ranking questions in his own videos and interviews. His emphasis shifts, so read these by date. Sends show up in all three.

- **May 2024:** “More important than watch time or like and comment counts is send rates.” And: “sends per reach correlate more, in my experience, with overall reach than anything else.”
- **June 2024**, in a long interview with Colin and Samir, his advice for creators was to look at “sends per reach.”
- **January 2025:** “The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends,” with sends “slightly more important” for reaching people who don't follow you.
