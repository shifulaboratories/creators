**The short version:** LinkedIn's feed tries to match posts to each member's professional interests, and in 2026 it said it's actively fighting pods, comment bots and bait. It rarely pays you directly. It pays you in clients, jobs, speaking gigs and brand deals with companies that sell to professionals.

## Expertise wins now

In March 2026 LinkedIn said its feed uses AI that reads what a post is actually about, and that it wants “genuine insight, actionable ideas, and thoughtful perspectives.” It also said it's working against the tricks people used to game it.

Carousels suit frameworks and breakdowns. LinkedIn newsletters notify subscribers each time you publish, which makes a weekly “what I learned” easy. Posts with outside links tend to get less engagement in Buffer's data, though LinkedIn hasn't said it penalizes them. Two to five posts a week is plenty.

## How you get paid

LinkedIn has no open ad-revenue program. The money is indirect, and often bigger than on consumer apps, because the audience has budgets.

| Route | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Your own business** | Consulting, courses, a newsletter, speaking and hiring all come in through DMs |
| **B2B brand deals** | Software, finance and recruiting brands pay to reach professionals. See [Brand deals 101](/deals/brand-deals/) |
| **BrandLink** | Since April 2025, brands sponsor video series from invited creators. LinkedIn hasn't published how creators are paid |

<DeepDive title="What LinkedIn has said, in its own words" hint="How the 2026 feed works, profile signals, crackdowns">

### What LinkedIn says it uses (March 2026)

- **AI that reads the post.** The feed uses "Generative Recommenders," augmented with large language models, to understand what a post is actually about and how each member's interests change over time.
- **Your profile is a signal.** What members share about themselves (industry, skills, experience, location) and how they engage over time shape what they see.
- **Relevance over popularity.** LinkedIn says it wants posts that offer "genuine insight, actionable ideas, and thoughtful perspectives."
- **Crackdowns:** comment automation, engagement pods, and unauthorized third-party tools, which LinkedIn says it's working to make ineffective.
- **New members** are being asked to pick interests at sign-up, so the feed has something to match from day one.

LinkedIn's editors have also described the feed in interviews. Buffer collected those quotes (December 2025): your connections and followers see your posts first, and knowledge-rich posts can travel beyond them.

BrandLink launch partners LinkedIn named: Steven Bartlett, Gary Vaynerchuk, Guy Raz and Allie K. Miller.
