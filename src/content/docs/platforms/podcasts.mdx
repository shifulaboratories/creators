## Shapes that work

- **Video interviews** with a guest who brings their own audience.
- **Solo explainers** that answer one searchable question per episode.
- **Audio-first shows** still work on Apple and Spotify, but leave the biggest screen (YouTube on a TV) empty.

- **Spotify Partner Program.** Launched January 2025 in the US, UK, Canada and Australia. Pays from Premium members watching your video, plus ads on Spotify Free. From April 2026, creators can swap and update host-read sponsorships in old episodes.
- **YouTube Partner Program.** Same thresholds and ad share as any channel. See [How you get paid on YouTube](/platforms/youtube/#how-you-get-paid).
- **Sponsorships.** Host-read ads are still the biggest earner for most shows, and they stay in your episodes on Spotify Premium.
- **Listener support** through paid subscriptions, memberships and Patreon-style bonus episodes. See [Paywalls and memberships](/earn/paywalls-and-memberships/).

<DeepDive title="What each app has said, in its own words" hint="YouTube, Spotify and Apple">

### What each app says it rewards

- **Apple Podcasts charts** use a mix of listening, follows and completion rate. Ratings, reviews and shares are "not factored into the algorithm," automatic downloads don't count, and chart spots can't be bought.
- **Apple's Trending Episodes** reflect "active, daily listening," so a strong launch week matters.
- **YouTube** recommends podcast episodes with the same system as every other video: clicks, watch time and satisfaction. See the [YouTube guide](/platforms/youtube/).
- **Spotify** says its short video clips are 33% more effective than previews at turning browsers into listeners.
