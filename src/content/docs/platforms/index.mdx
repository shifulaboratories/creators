Every platform has published at least one explanation of how its recommendations work. These guides start from what the platform itself says, then add what practitioners observe, clearly labeled.

<GuideGrid section="platforms" kicker="Platform guide" />

## At a glance

| Platform | What it says matters most | What it says does not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [YouTube](/platforms/youtube/) | Clicks, watch time, and whether viewers say they were satisfied | It does not rely on your social graph |
| [TikTok](/platforms/tiktok/) | How people interact with your video, especially watching a longer video to the end | Follower count and past hits are not direct factors |
| [Instagram](/platforms/instagram/) | Different signals for Feed, Explore and Reels; for Reels, reshares and watching all the way through | It says it does not suppress posts to sell ads |
| [X](/platforms/x/) | Predicted engagement: likes, replies and reposts | Not stated |

:::note[How to read these guides]
Claims marked **From the platform** come from the platform's own published explanation, linked at the end of each guide. Claims marked **Creator-tested** are what experienced creators report, and can change.
:::
