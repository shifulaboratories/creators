**The short version:** Facebook ranks posts from thousands of signals, mostly what people like, comment on and watch. Since 2025 Meta has been openly rewarding original content and cutting reach and pay for accounts that repost other people's work. Pay comes through one program, Content Monetization, which is invite-only, and Creator Fast Track guarantees three months of pay to creators who already have 100,000 followers elsewhere.

## What Facebook says it uses

From the platform, 2026:

- **Engagement signals.** Meta says its ranking learns "from the posts you like and comment on to what you watch (among thousands of other signals)." Its engineers describe the traditional system as one that has "learned from implicit engagement signals," meaning what you do rather than what you say. *(From the platform.)*
- **What viewers tell it directly.** Since October 2026, Facebook's **Your algorithm** lets people add and remove interests for Feed and Reels. Meta's engineers say those choices are applied at every stage, from picking candidates through reranking. Topics marked "not interested" get demoted, and if someone names a creator they don't want, that creator's posts are filtered out for them. *(From the platform.)*
- **A cap on any one topic.** The same system spaces out recommendations for a single interest and limits how much of someone's day one interest can take up. *(From the platform.)*
- **Originality.** In March 2026 Meta said views and time spent on original Reels "approximately doubled" in the second half of 2025 compared with a year earlier. *(From the platform.)*

## What counts as original, and what gets cut

Meta defines original content as content "filmed or produced directly by a creator or owner of a Profile or Page" (March 2026).

- Accounts that keep reusing other people's content **lose monetization for a period and get less reach on everything they post**, not only the copied posts (July 2025). *(From the platform.)*
- Accounts that are mostly unoriginal may be "deemed non-recommendable and demonetized" (March 2026). There is an appeal route in Meta's Business Help Center. *(From the platform.)*
- Meta says "simply stitching together clips or adding your watermark does not qualify as meaningful enhancement." *(From the platform.)*
- In the first half of 2025 Meta took action on about 500,000 accounts for spammy behavior or fake engagement, and removed about 10 million profiles impersonating big creators. *(From the platform.)*

## What to optimize for

- **Post your own originals, natively.** Meta says Pages and profiles get "the greatest distribution when they consist primarily of original content." If you repurpose from TikTok or Instagram, post the clean file you made, not a download of a repost. *(From the platform, applied.)*
- **Make posts people comment on and watch through.** Likes, comments and watching are the signals Meta names first. *(From the platform.)*
- **Stay in a lane people can name.** Viewers can now add or remove topics. A clear niche makes you easy to pick, and a "not interested" in your topic hits everything you post on it. *(From the platform, applied.)*
- **Never repost to fill a calendar.** One account-level penalty cuts reach on your original posts too. *(From the platform.)*

## Formats that work

- **Reels**, short or long. Reels made up 60% of Content Monetization payouts in 2025, per Meta. *(From the platform.)*
- **Photos and text posts** also earn under Content Monetization, which is unusual among the big platforms. *(From the platform.)*
- **Stories** count toward payouts as well. *(From the platform.)*
- **Commentary and reactions** work only if you add a point of view. Watching along to someone else's video is named as unoriginal. *(From the platform.)*

## How you get paid

Meta says Facebook paid creators **nearly $3 billion in 2025**, up 35% on the year before.

- **Content Monetization** is Facebook's single creator program. It pays for Reels (short and long), Stories, photos and text posts, based on how they perform. Meta doesn't publish a rate or a follower minimum. *(From the platform.)*
- **Creator Fast Track** is for creators who are big somewhere else. You get three months of guaranteed pay for posting eligible Reels on Facebook, extra reach on those Reels, and immediate access to Content Monetization. *(From the platform.)*
- **College Edition** (added Nov 13, 2026): US college creators with 1,000+ followers on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube can apply with a .edu email. Meta lists bonuses from $100 to $450 a month starting at 20,000 followers, then the $1,000 and $3,000 tiers. *(From the platform.)*
- **What gets you cut off:** reposting other people's content and spam tactics both end monetization. *(From the platform.)*
- **Brand deals** still matter most for most creators. See [Brand deals](/deals/).
