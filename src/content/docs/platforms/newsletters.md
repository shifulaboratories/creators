**The short version:** Every other guide here is about renting attention from an algorithm. A newsletter is the audience you own: if a platform bans you or changes the rules, your list still works. The newer twist is that newsletter platforms now have their own discovery networks, so you don't have to grow from zero.

## How newsletters get discovered now

- **Platform networks.** Substack says its network drives 25% of paid subscriptions on the platform, and more than 25% of subscriptions start in its apps. *(From the platform, checked Nov 2026.)*
- **Recommendations.** On Substack, writers recommend each other's publications, and Notes works like a social feed inside the app. *(From the platform.)*
- **Your social accounts.** Most writers still grow by pointing their social audience to the sign-up page. *(Creator-tested.)*

## What to optimize for

- **One clear promise.** "What you'll get every Tuesday" in one line, on the sign-up page and every social bio. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Opens and replies.** Inbox providers watch whether people open, reply or mark you as spam. Ask a question people want to answer. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Cross-promotion with peers.** Recommending writers your readers would like is how the networks grow both of you. *(From the platform, applied.)*
- **A schedule you can keep.** Consistency is what makes people pay. *(Creator-tested.)*

## How you get paid: Substack vs beehiiv

**A quick way to choose:** a flat fee beats a percentage once you're earning enough. At beehiiv's $95 Pro plan, Substack's 10% costs more once paid revenue passes about $950 a month. Below that, Substack's free start and network are hard to beat. *(Our math from their published prices.)*

Other ways newsletters earn:

- **Sponsorships.** One sponsor slot per issue, priced per thousand opens. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Ad networks** like beehiiv's, which match you with advertisers automatically. *(From the platform.)*
- **Products.** Courses, templates and guides sold to the people who already read you. *(Creator-tested.)*
