**The short version:** Threads ranks posts by predicting whether you'll like them, open the replies, tap the profile, follow the author, or scroll right past. It's a conversation app tied to your Instagram account. Over 400 million people use it monthly, but Meta doesn't pay creators for Threads posts directly right now.

## What Meta says it uses

From the platform (Meta's explainer, as reproduced by Buffer, January 2026):

- **Two feeds.** Following is reverse-chronological and shows only accounts you follow. For You mixes accounts you follow with others "we think you'll enjoy."
- **An AI system picks and orders For You.** "Content that the system predicts will provide more value for you is shown higher in your feed."
- **Five predictions:** how likely you are to like a post, view its replies, follow the author, click the author's profile, and scroll past it.
- **Inputs include** posts you've seen and liked, others' reply clicks, profile clicks, recently followed accounts, and your **Instagram profile views**. Threads and Instagram share signals.
- **Instagram's Community Guidelines** apply. Posts predicted to break them are less likely to be recommended.

## What to optimize for

- **Start conversations.** "Will they open the replies?" is one of only five predictions, so posts that invite real answers are built for this feed. *(From the platform, applied.)*
- **Reply to your replies.** Buffer's analysis of 128,000 posts found replying to comments lifted engagement 42%. *(Creator-tested, Buffer data.)*
- **Make your profile worth the tap.** Profile clicks and follows are both predicted, and Instagram profile views feed in too. A clear bio and a strong Instagram grid help both apps. *(From the platform, applied.)*
- **Use one topic tag per post** to join a running conversation. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Don't post what people scroll past.** "Scroll past" is the one prediction that counts against you. *(From the platform.)*

## Formats that work

- **Questions and hot takes** in your niche that people want to answer. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Behind-the-scenes text** that would be too casual for an Instagram post. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Cross-posts from Instagram** with a line of context, since the two apps share an account. *(Creator-tested.)*

## How you get paid

- **No direct payouts today.** Meta began showing ads to all Threads users worldwide in January 2026, but TechCrunch's report on the rollout mentions no revenue sharing for creators. *(Reporting.)*
- **Threads pays you through Instagram.** Followers you earn on Threads see you on Instagram too, where Subscriptions, Gifts, Bonuses and brand deals live. See the [Instagram guide](/platforms/instagram/). *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Brands buy Threads posts as part of Instagram packages.** Price it as an add-on, not a standalone deliverable. See [Pricing](/deals/pricing/). *(Creator-tested.)*
