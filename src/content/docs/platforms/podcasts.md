## What each app says it rewards

- **Apple Podcasts charts** use a mix of listening, follows and completion rate. Ratings, reviews and shares are "not factored into the algorithm," automatic downloads don't count, and chart spots can't be bought. *(From the platform.)*
- **Apple's Trending Episodes** reflect "active, daily listening," so a strong launch week matters. *(From the platform.)*
- **YouTube** recommends podcast episodes with the same system as every other video: clicks, watch time and satisfaction. See the [YouTube guide](/platforms/youtube/). *(From the platform.)*
- **Spotify** says its short video clips are 33% more effective than previews at turning browsers into listeners. *(From the platform, January 2025.)*

## What to optimize for

- **Follows and finishes on Apple.** Ask for the follow, not the review, since reviews don't move the chart. Make episodes people complete. *(From the platform, applied.)*
- **Titles and thumbnails on YouTube.** An episode is a video there. Name it after the most interesting thing said, not "Episode 47." *(Creator-tested, consistent with YouTube's signals.)*
- **Clips everywhere.** Cut the best two minutes into Shorts, Reels, TikToks and Spotify clips. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Launch with a few episodes.** Spotify's program now needs only three, and a back catalog gives new fans more to finish. *(From the platform, applied.)*

## Formats that work

- **Video interviews** with a guest who brings their own audience. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Solo explainers** that answer one searchable question per episode. *(Creator-tested.)*
- **Audio-first shows** still work on Apple and Spotify, but leave the biggest screen (YouTube on a TV) empty. *(Creator-tested.)*

- **Spotify Partner Program.** Launched January 2025 in the US, UK, Canada and Australia. Pays from Premium members watching your video, plus ads on Spotify Free. From April 2026, creators can swap and update host-read sponsorships in old episodes. *(From the platform.)*
- **YouTube Partner Program.** Same thresholds and ad share as any channel. See [How you get paid on YouTube](/platforms/youtube/#how-you-get-paid). *(From the platform.)*
- **Sponsorships.** Host-read ads are still the biggest earner for most shows, and they stay in your episodes on Spotify Premium. *(From the platform, plus creator-tested.)*
- **Listener support** through paid subscriptions, memberships and Patreon-style bonus episodes. See [Paywalls and memberships](/earn/paywalls-and-memberships/). *(Creator-tested.)*
