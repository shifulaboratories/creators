**The short version:** Threads ranks posts by predicting whether you'll like them, open the replies, tap the profile, follow the author, or scroll right past. It's a conversation app tied to your Instagram account. Over 400 million people use it monthly, but Meta doesn't pay creators for Threads posts directly right now.

## What to post

- **Questions and hot takes** in your niche that people want to answer.
- **Behind-the-scenes text** that would be too casual for an Instagram post.
- **Cross-posts from Instagram** with a line of context, since the two apps share an account.

## How it pays (through Instagram)

- **No direct payouts today.** Meta began showing ads to all Threads users worldwide in January 2026, but TechCrunch's report on the rollout mentions no revenue sharing for creators.
- **Threads pays you through Instagram.** Followers you earn on Threads see you on Instagram too, where Subscriptions, Gifts, Bonuses and brand deals live. See the [Instagram guide](/platforms/instagram/).
- **Brands buy Threads posts as part of Instagram packages.** Price it as an add-on, not a standalone deliverable. See [Pricing](/deals/pricing/).

### What Meta says it uses

From the platform (Meta's explainer, as reproduced by Buffer, January 2026):

- **Two feeds.** Following is reverse-chronological and shows only accounts you follow. For You mixes accounts you follow with others "we think you'll enjoy."
- **An AI system picks and orders For You.** "Content that the system predicts will provide more value for you is shown higher in your feed."
- **Five predictions:** how likely you are to like a post, view its replies, follow the author, click the author's profile, and scroll past it.
- **Inputs include** posts you've seen and liked, others' reply clicks, profile clicks, recently followed accounts, and your **Instagram profile views**. Threads and Instagram share signals.
- **Instagram's Community Guidelines** apply. Posts predicted to break them are less likely to be recommended.
