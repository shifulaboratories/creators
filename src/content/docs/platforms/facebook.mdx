**The short version:** Facebook ranks posts from thousands of signals, mostly what people like, comment on and watch. Since 2025 Meta has been openly rewarding original content and cutting reach and pay for accounts that repost other people's work. Pay comes through one program, Content Monetization, which is invite-only, and Creator Fast Track guarantees three months of pay to creators who already have 100,000 followers elsewhere.

## Original beats everything

Since 2025, Meta has been openly rewarding original content and cutting accounts that repost. In March 2026 it said views and time on original Reels “approximately doubled” in the second half of 2025. Original means “filmed or produced directly by a creator or owner of a Profile or Page.”

Meta says “simply stitching together clips or adding your watermark does not qualify as meaningful enhancement.” Accounts that are mostly unoriginal may be “deemed non-recommendable and demonetized.” There is an appeal route in Meta's Business Help Center.

## Give the feed what it reads

Meta's ranking learns “from the posts you like and comment on to what you watch (among thousands of other signals).” And since October 2026, viewers can tell Facebook directly which topics they want more or less of.

## Every format can earn

Unusually, Facebook pays for more than video: Reels (short or long), Stories, photos and text posts all count toward Content Monetization. Reels made up 60% of payouts in 2025. Commentary and reactions work only if you add a point of view.

## How you get paid

Meta says Facebook paid creators **nearly $3 billion in 2025**, up 35% on the year before.

- **Content Monetization** is Facebook's single creator program. It pays for Reels (short and long), Stories, photos and text posts, based on how they perform. Meta doesn't publish a rate or a follower minimum.
- **Creator Fast Track** is for creators who are big somewhere else. You get three months of guaranteed pay for posting eligible Reels on Facebook, extra reach on those Reels, and immediate access to Content Monetization.
- **College Edition** (added Nov 13, 2026): US college creators with 1,000+ followers on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube can apply with a .edu email. Meta lists bonuses from $100 to $450 a month starting at 20,000 followers, then the $1,000 and $3,000 tiers.
- **What gets you cut off:** reposting other people's content and spam tactics both end monetization.
- **Brand deals** still matter most for most creators. See [Brand deals](/deals/).

<DeepDive title="What Meta has said, in its own words" hint="Ranking signals, Your algorithm, enforcement numbers">

### What Facebook says it uses

- **Engagement signals.** Meta says its ranking learns "from the posts you like and comment on to what you watch (among thousands of other signals)." Its engineers describe the traditional system as one that has "learned from implicit engagement signals," meaning what you do rather than what you say.
- **What viewers tell it directly.** Since October 2026, Facebook's **Your algorithm** lets people add and remove interests for Feed and Reels. Meta's engineers say those choices are applied at every stage, from picking candidates through reranking. Topics marked "not interested" get demoted, and if someone names a creator they don't want, that creator's posts are filtered out for them.
- **A cap on any one topic.** The same system spaces out recommendations for a single interest and limits how much of someone's day one interest can take up.
- **Originality.** In March 2026 Meta said views and time spent on original Reels "approximately doubled" in the second half of 2025 compared with a year earlier.

### Enforcement

- Accounts that keep reusing other people's content **lose monetization for a period and get less reach on everything they post**, not only the copied posts (July 2025).
- In the first half of 2025 Meta took action on about 500,000 accounts for spammy behavior or fake engagement, and removed about 10 million profiles impersonating big creators.
