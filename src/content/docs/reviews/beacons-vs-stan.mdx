**The short answer:** Under about $320 a month in sales, Beacons Free costs less. Above that, a flat plan wins, and Stan Creator ($29) and Beacons Creator Plus ($30) cost almost the same. Billed yearly, both are $300.

<CostCompare slugs={["beacons", "stan-store"]} />

## Where the line is

Beacons Free takes 9% of each sale. Stan Creator costs $29 a month and takes 0%. They cost the same at about **$322 a month** in sales (29 ÷ 0.09). Past that, Stan is cheaper than Beacons Free, but Beacons Creator Plus ($30, 0%) is then just $1 a month more than Stan, and both cost $300 a year billed yearly. Card processing is the same Stripe 2.9% + $0.30 on both, so it doesn’t change the answer.

## Pick Beacons if

- You haven’t sold anything yet and want to start at $0.
- You want a media kit, email list and brand outreach in the same app.

## Pick Stan if

- You want a store built around phone checkout and nothing else to learn.
- You’re fine paying from day one after a 14-day trial.
