**The short answer:** Stan is a store first, at a published $29 a month with 0% fees. Linktree is a link page first, with a free plan; Pro also sells with 0% Linktree fees, but check its live price.

<CostCompare slugs={["linktree", "stan-store"]} />

## Where the line is

On fees they’re close: Stan takes 0% on every plan, and Linktree takes 0% on Pro and Premium. Both add Stripe’s 2.9% + $0.30. The difference is price and focus. Stan publishes $29 a month (Creator) and $99 (Creator Pro), with no free plan. Linktree has a free plan but doesn’t list Pro’s price in its help center, and on Free and Starter its selling fee “depends on your plan”.

## Pick Linktree if

- You mostly share links and sell now and then.
- You want to stay free until selling is worth paying for.

## Pick Stan if

- Selling is the main job of your bio link.
- You want a published, flat price you can plan around.
