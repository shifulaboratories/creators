  - q: "How much does Stan Store cost?"

  - q: "Does Stan take a percentage of sales?"
    a: "No. Stan’s help center says it charges 0% on every plan. You still pay card processing: Stripe’s 2.9% + $0.30 per US sale, or PayPal’s 3.49% + a fixed fee, plus Stripe’s payout fee."

    a: "Only once you sell. At $300 a month in sales, a 9% fee elsewhere costs $27, about the same as Stan’s $29. Below that, a free plan with a percentage fee is cheaper. Above it, Stan’s flat price wins."

    a: "No. Per Stan’s help center, card processing fees aren’t returned when you refund a buyer."

## What it costs at real sales

| Monthly sales | Cheapest plan | Total cost | You keep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $300 (15 orders) | Creator | $42.20 | $257.80 |
| $1,000 (40 orders) | Creator | $70 | $930 |
| $5,000 (150 orders) | Creator | $219 | $4,781 |

Total cost is the monthly plan price, Stan’s cut, and card processing (2.9% + $0.30 per sale), billed monthly. Yearly billing is cheaper where offered.

## Good fit if

- You already sell something, or will soon, and want one tidy page for products, calls and courses.
- Your sales are past roughly $320 a month, where a flat $29 beats a 9% cut.
- Most of your buyers come from your phone audience, not from search or a marketplace.

## Pick something else if

- You’re testing an idea and don’t want a bill before your first sale: Beacons Free or Gumroad cost $0 a month.
- You want buyers to find you inside a marketplace: Stan doesn’t advertise one.
