## What it costs at real sales

| Monthly sales | Cheapest plan | Total cost | You keep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $300 (15 orders) | Free | $40.20 | $259.80 |
| $1,000 (40 orders) | Creator Plus | $71 | $929 |
| $5,000 (150 orders) | Creator Plus | $220 | $4,780 |

Total cost is the monthly plan price, Beacons’s cut, and card processing (2.9% + $0.30 per sale), billed monthly. Yearly billing is cheaper where offered.

## Good fit if

- You’re starting out and want a link in bio, store and media kit for $0.
- You want email and brand outreach tools in the same app.
- You’ll upgrade to Creator Plus once you sell about $333 a month.

## Pick something else if

- You only need a simple link page and a checkout: you may pay for features you don’t use.
- You send more than 50 emails a month and don’t want to pay: the Free plan caps sends at 50.
