## What it costs at real sales

We can’t work this out honestly yet. Linktree’s help center doesn’t list plan prices or the selling fee on its Free and Starter plans, and its pricing page changes by region. What is published: Pro and Premium sell with 0% Linktree fees, and Stripe takes 2.9% + $0.30 per purchase on every plan. ## Good fit if

- You mainly need a link page, and selling is a side feature.
- You’re already on Linktree and want to sell without moving: Pro advertises 0% Linktree fees.
- You want the most familiar link page to your audience.

## Pick something else if

- You want to compare costs before you sign up: Linktree’s help center doesn’t list plan prices or the fee on Free and Starter.
- Selling is your main goal: store-first apps like Stan and Beacons have more selling tools.
