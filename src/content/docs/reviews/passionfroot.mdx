A storefront where brands book and pay for your sponsorship packages, plus a network that matches you with brands. Strongest for B2B, newsletters and podcasts.

## What it takes from a deal

| Deal | Your own deals | Network deals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $300 | You keep $300 | You keep $255 ($45 fee) |
| $1,000 | You keep $1,000 | You keep $850 ($150 fee) |
| $5,000 | You keep $5,000 | You keep $4,250 ($750 fee) |

Fees from your payout only. The brand-side fee on your own deals is paid by the brand, on top of your price.

## Good fit if

- Brands already reach out to you and you want a clean way to sell packages and get paid.
- You run a newsletter, podcast or B2B channel, where Passionfroot is strongest.
- You want to keep 100% of deals you bring in.

## Pick something else if

- You need a marketplace to find your first brands: network deals cost 15%, including repeat bookings from that brand.
- You need payouts by bank transfer in a currency other than USD.
