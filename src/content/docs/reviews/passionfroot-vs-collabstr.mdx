**The short answer:** If brands already come to you, Passionfroot lets you keep 100% of those deals. If you need a marketplace to land your first deals, Collabstr’s 15% buys you buyers and escrow.

## Where the line is

On a $1,000 deal that you bring in yourself, Passionfroot takes $0 from you (the brand pays a 5% fee on top), and Collabstr takes $150. On deals the platform finds for you, both take 15%: $150 on $1,000. Collabstr’s deals skew small, with nearly 80% of its 2025 deals under $300, while Passionfroot is strongest for newsletters, podcasts and B2B.

## Pick Passionfroot if

- Brands already email you and you want a booking page.
- You run a newsletter, podcast or B2B channel.

## Pick Collabstr if

- You want a marketplace where brands search for creators.
- You want escrow and fast, small deals while you build a portfolio.
