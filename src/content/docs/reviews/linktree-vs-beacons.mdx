**The short answer:** If you want to sell and know your costs upfront, Beacons publishes every price and fee. If you mainly share links, Linktree’s free plan is fine; check its live Pro price before selling, since Pro sells with 0% Linktree fees.

<CostCompare slugs={["linktree", "beacons"]} />

## Where the line is

Beacons publishes everything: Free and Creator take 9% of each sale, Creator Plus ($30 a month) takes 0%. Linktree’s Pro and Premium plans sell with 0% Linktree fees, but its help center doesn’t list plan prices or the fee on Free and Starter. So the honest comparison is Beacons Creator Plus at $30 a month against Linktree Pro at whatever the live price is in your region. Both add Stripe’s 2.9% + $0.30 per sale.

## Pick Linktree if

- You mainly need a link page your audience already recognizes.
- You’ll check Linktree’s live Pro price and it beats $30 a month.

## Pick Beacons if

- You want a free store and media kit with published fees.
- You want email and brand outreach tools in the same place.
