## What it takes from a deal

| Deal | Creator |
| --- | --- |
| $300 | You keep $255 ($45 fee) |
| $1,000 | You keep $850 ($150 fee) |
| $5,000 | You keep $4,250 ($750 fee) |

Fees from your payout only. Payout method fees, if any, are extra.

## Good fit if

- You’re under 100K followers and want your first paid deals or UGC work.
- You want the brand’s money held in escrow before you start.
- You’re happy to take smaller deals while you build a portfolio.

## Pick something else if

- Brands already come to you directly: a storefront where you keep 100% costs you less.
- You only take large deals: nearly 80% of 2025 Collabstr deals were under $300, per Collabstr.
