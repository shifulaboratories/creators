**The short answer:** Gumroad is cheaper while you sell less than about $190 to $280 a month (depending on your price). Past that, Stan’s flat $29 costs less, and the gap grows fast.

<CostCompare slugs={["stan-store", "gumroad"]} />

## Where the line is

Gumroad takes 10% + $0.50 per sale. Stan takes $29 a month and nothing per sale. Where they meet depends on your average price: at **$10** products, about **$193** a month in sales; at **$25**, about **$242**; at **$100**, about **$276**. Processing (2.9% + $0.30) is on top for both, so it doesn’t move the line. At $5,000 a month with 150 orders, Gumroad totals about $765 against Stan’s $219.

## Pick Stan if

- You want to start with no monthly bill.
- You sell occasionally, or want buyers to find you in Gumroad’s Discover marketplace.

## Pick Gumroad if

- You sell every week and want a flat price.
- You sell coaching, calls and courses from your bio link.
