**The short answer:** Beacons Free costs less than Gumroad at every sale size, because 9% is less than 10% + $0.50. Gumroad’s advantage is its Discover marketplace, where buyers can find you.

<CostCompare slugs={["beacons", "gumroad"]} />

## Where the line is

Both cost $0 a month. Beacons Free takes 9% per sale; Gumroad takes 10% + $0.50. So Beacons Free is cheaper on every sale. On a $10 product, Beacons takes $0.90 and Gumroad $1.50, before the same card processing. Once you sell about $333 a month, Beacons Creator Plus ($30, 0%) is cheaper still. Gumroad’s rate falls to 5% + $0.50 only after $20,000 in sales in a month.

## Pick Beacons if

- You want a link in bio, media kit and store together.
- You care most about keeping more of each sale.

## Pick Gumroad if

- You want buyers to find you in a marketplace, and accept 30% on those sales.
- You want Gumroad to return its fee when you refund.
