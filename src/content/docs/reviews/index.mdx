## How we review

- **Prices from the source.** Every plan price and fee comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help center, linked on the card with the date we checked.
- **Total cost, not sticker price.** We add the plan, the tool’s cut of each sale and card processing, because that’s what leaves your account.
- **No affiliate links, no paid placement.** Nobody paid to be here, and the order comes from the math.
- **Open to correction.** The data lives in one file on [GitHub](https://github.com/shifulaboratories/creators/blob/main/src/lib/reviews-data.ts). If a price changes, [open an issue](https://github.com/shifulaboratories/creators/issues).
