## Good fit if

- You’re selling your first product and want nothing to pay until a sale happens.
- You sell occasionally, so a monthly bill would cost more than the fees.
- You want buyers to find you through Gumroad’s Discover marketplace and accept its 30% cut there.

## Pick something else if

- You sell more than about $250 a month: a $29 or $30 flat plan elsewhere costs less.
- You sell cheap products: on a $10 sale, Gumroad and processing take about $2.09.
