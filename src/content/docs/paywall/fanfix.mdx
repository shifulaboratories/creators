**The short version:** Fanfix is a paid fan club for creators who don’t make adult content. Fans subscribe, tip, unlock posts and pay to message you. You keep 80%, and nudity isn’t allowed, which makes it one of the few fan platforms a brand partner won’t think twice about.

## The rules that matter

<span class="sc-tag is-platform">From the platform</span> From Fanfix’s creator terms, last updated Aug 1, 2026:

- **Your cut:** creators get 80% of what fans pay directly (memberships, direct messages, locked content). Fanfix keeps 20%.
- **Payouts:** about 30 days after each calendar month ends, unless another schedule applies to you.
- **Age:** you must be 18 or older. A parent’s permission doesn’t change that.
- **Managers and agencies:** if an agent or agency runs your account, you’re still personally responsible for everything on it.
- **AI content:** you can’t show a real person’s likeness without their permission, and Fanfix may label or remove AI content.
- **Competing platforms:** you can’t use your Fanfix account to promote other monetization platforms, which the terms name as including Patreon and OnlyFans. Breaking this can cost you your earnings.

<span class="sc-tag is-platform">Reported</span> TechCrunch reported in 2023 that Fanfix allows no nudity and that the platform, including private messages, is human-moderated.

## Who it suits

- Creators whose audience is young and wants closeness: DMs, voice notes, behind-the-scenes, “first to know.”
- Lifestyle, beauty, fashion, fitness, comedy and music creators who want fan income without an adult label.
- Creators who also do brand deals and want a link in bio every brand is comfortable with. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>

## The catches

- **20% is the same cut as OnlyFans.** Patreon and Ko-fi take less, if you don’t need paid DMs.
- **Exclusivity.** The competing-platforms rule means Fanfix works best as your one fan platform.
- **You bring the fans.** Like every platform here, it won’t grow your audience for you.
- **Agency pitches.** Fanfix’s popularity means agencies pitch Fanfix creators too. The same rules apply: read [chatting agencies](/paywall/chatting-agencies/) and [signing with an agency](/paywall/agency-red-flags/) first.

## Background

Fanfix launched in 2021, founded by Harry Gestetner and Simon Pompan with creator Cameron Dallas, per TechCrunch and The Hustle. It was bought by SuperOrdinary in 2022 and in August 2025 acquired Sunroom, a creator platform aimed at women, per Wikipedia’s citations.
