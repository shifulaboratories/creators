**The short version:** if what fans want from you is *more of your work* (bonus episodes, ad-free videos, tutorials, art files), Patreon or Ko-fi usually beats a DM-first fan platform. They take less, and they read as a membership, not a fan club.

## Patreon

Pages launched after Aug 4, 2025 pay a standard 10% platform fee plus payment processing (2.9% + $0.30 per US card payment). Older pages may be on 5%, 8% or 11% plans. Members who join through Patreon’s iPhone app can cost you up to 30%; US members can join on the mobile website instead. You can export your member list.

Best for podcasters, YouTubers, artists, writers and educators with a steady output of bonus work. Read [Paywalls and memberships](/earn/paywalls-and-memberships/) for tiers, pricing and what to put behind the wall.

## Ko-fi

In Free mode, Ko-fi takes 0% on one-off tips and 5% on memberships, monthly tips, commissions and shop sales. Standard mode charges a flat 5% on everything with no monthly fee. PayPal or Stripe add their own fee, usually around 3% + $0.30.

Best for artists and smaller creators who want a tip jar, a few membership tiers and a simple shop in one place.

## When to pick a fan platform instead

If your fans mainly want to talk to *you* (replies, voice notes, personal shout-outs), Patreon and Ko-fi’s lack of paid messages leaves money on the table. That’s what [Fanfix](/paywall/fanfix/) and the others are built for.
