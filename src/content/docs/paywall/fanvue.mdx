## The rules that matter

<span class="sc-tag is-platform">From the platform</span>

- **Earning rate.** 80% of what fans pay, unless Fanvue agrees otherwise in writing. It can offer a temporary promotional rate and withdraw it at any time. The 85% rate offered to creators who joined in 2022 has ended.
- **Payouts.** Withdraw any time once a payment is 7 days old and you’re over the minimum. Fanvue aims to process within 10 business days, and you have 30 days to report a problem.
- **Chargebacks.** If more than 1.5% of your sales are disputed over 30 days, Fanvue may charge $50 per further dispute.
- **Stay on the platform.** You can’t ask for or accept payment outside Fanvue or promote outside payment links. Doing so can mean suspension, withheld payouts or removal.
- **Consent.** Anyone else who appears in your content needs documented consent, including for their likeness.
- **Onboarding.** ID verification with a liveness check, tax and bank details, and a declaration of whether your content is explicit.

## Who it suits

- Creators who want OnlyFans-style tools on a smaller platform.
- Creators using AI tools in their content, as long as they follow the likeness and consent rules.
- Anyone who has been offered a referral deal: Fanvue’s terms say referral commissions are paid from its own fee, not from the referred creator’s earnings.

## The catches

- **Same 20% as OnlyFans,** now that the launch rate is gone.
- **Smaller audience** than OnlyFans, and like every platform here, it won’t find fans for you. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
- **Adult label.** As with OnlyFans, brand partners may read a Fanvue link as adult.
