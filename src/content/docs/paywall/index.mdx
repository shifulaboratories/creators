A paywall platform is a paid fan club. Fans subscribe monthly, tip, unlock posts or pay to message you, and the platform takes a cut. It’s one of the few kinds of creator income that doesn’t depend on a brand or an algorithm, which is why so many creators run one next to their free content.

Three things decide how much you make: <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>

1. **How many people care enough to pay.** A few percent of a loyal audience beats a tiny share of a huge, passive one.
2. **What they get for it.** A clear promise (“a behind-the-scenes vlog every Sunday”) keeps people subscribed. Vague “exclusive content” doesn’t.
3. **How you handle messages.** On most fan platforms a big part of income comes from paid messages and tips, which is why a whole industry of chatting agencies exists. Read how they work before you hire one.
