## What creators actually use

Real setups from beauty, lifestyle, business, music, podcast and film creators, taken only from gear pages they published or setup videos they filmed themselves. Each one is dated, because gear changes fast. Tap a niche to find someone who makes what you make, or open the [creator directory](/creators/) to see their apps and advice too.

**The pattern across all of them:** sound and light come before the camera. A ring light did most of the work for Keiko Lynn's beauty shots, Matt D'Avella lights with a paper lantern, and Tim Ferriss runs a top podcast on a field recorder and two classic mics.
