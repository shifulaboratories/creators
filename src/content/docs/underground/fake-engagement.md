**The short version:** bought followers and likes come from a wholesale chain of "panels," some powered by hijacked devices. They're cheap because they're fake. Meta has sued sellers and won, and since 2024 the FTC bans buying or selling fake indicators of social influence for commercial purposes.

## How the panel chain works

Security researchers Masarah Paquet-Clouston and Olivier Bilodeau (GoSecure) mapped the business in a 2018 Virus Bulletin paper.

<li><strong>Reseller panels</strong><span>Wholesale dashboards that link to cheaper panels upstream. The researchers counted about 1,500 of them, plus five companies selling the panel software itself.</span></li>

The markup is the business. In the paper, the median price for 1,000 Instagram followers was about $1 at reseller panels, against an estimated $13 at customer-facing sellers.

## Meta has sued, and won

- **March 2019:** Meta sued four companies and three people based in China over selling fake accounts, likes and followers.
- **April 2019:** Meta sued a New Zealand company and three people over selling fake likes, views and followers on Instagram.
- **November 2022:** a US federal judge granted Meta a default judgment of about $200,000 against the alleged operator of a fake-engagement service. Meta said its accounts were "responsible for over 8 million likes over the course of just two days," according to The Register.

## Why it hurts the buyer

- **It wrecks your own signals.** Platforms rank on what viewers do. Followers who never watch, send or comment drag down every rate the feed looks at, so your real posts get tested on dead air. See [how a post snowballs](/algorithms/why-feeds-exist/).
- **Brands check.** An audience that doesn't engage is easy to spot, and it can end a deal.
- **It's a legal risk in the US.** The FTC's 2024 rule bans buying or selling fake indicators of social media influence for commercial purposes.

## If this happened to you

- **Someone bought fake followers for your account to get it flagged:** document it, report it to the platform, and don't try to "balance it out" by buying more.
- **You bought followers in the past:** stop, and let them fall off. Don't use a "cleanup" service; that's the same industry.
