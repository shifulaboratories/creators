**The short version:** a brand deal has six steps, and you can run every one of them yourself. Pick the one you’re on below. You don’t need a manager to get a fair deal, you need to know your number, price rights separately, and fix the five or six clauses that carry the money and the risk. Brands expect creators to push back, so asking politely and in writing is normal business.
