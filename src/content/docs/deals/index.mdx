**The short version:** you don’t need a manager to get a fair deal, you need to know which five or six clauses carry the money and the risk. Brands expect creators to push back on payment terms, usage rights and exclusivity. Asking politely, in writing, is normal business, not being difficult.

:::caution[Not legal advice]
These guides are educational. Laws differ by state and country. For a large fee, a long term, anything involving your likeness or AI, or wording you don’t understand, have a lawyer review the contract before you sign.
:::
