## Red-flag words to search for

Before you read a contract line by line, search it (Ctrl+F or Find on your phone) for these. Each one usually signals a clause worth a second look: <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>

| Search for | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **perpetuity**, **perpetual**, **irrevocable** | Rights that never end. SAG-AFTRA advises avoiding “in perpetuity” when possible. |
| **hereafter devised**, **throughout the universe** | Usage in every format that will ever exist, anywhere |
| **work made for hire**, **assign** | You give up ownership of your own content |
| **sole discretion** | The brand decides alone: approvals, morality, termination |
| **including but not limited to** | A list that isn’t really a limit |
| **derivative works**, **likeness**, **voice** | Reuse of you beyond the post, including AI |
| **indemnify**, **hold harmless** | Who pays if someone sues |
| **net 60**, **net 90**, **receipt of payment** | Slow or conditional payment |
| **automatically renew**, **option to extend** | The deal continues unless you act |

## When to bring in a lawyer

<span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>

- The fee is big enough that losing it would hurt.
- The term or exclusivity runs longer than a few months.
- It mentions your likeness, voice, digital replicas or AI.
- It’s an ambassador or equity deal, or it sets up an ongoing relationship.
- You don’t understand a clause after reading it twice.

Many entertainment lawyers will review a single contract for a flat fee. If you’re on a SAG-AFTRA Influencer Agreement, the union has resources too.
