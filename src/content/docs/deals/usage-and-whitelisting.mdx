**The short version:** your fee pays for the post. Everything the brand does with that post afterwards, reposting it, putting it on their site, running it as an ad, is a right you’re licensing. Price each right by where it runs and for how long, keep ownership, and give ad access through the platform’s own tools with an end date.

## Know the three things a brand can buy

<figure class="sc-figure">
<ol class="sc-steps">
<li class="is-active"><strong>The post</strong><span>You make it and post it on your channels. This is your base fee.</span></li>
<li><strong>Usage</strong><span>They repost or reuse it on their own channels, site and email, for a set time.</span></li>
<li><strong>Paid ads</strong><span>They put money behind it, from their account or from yours (“whitelisting”).</span></li>
</ol>
<figcaption>Each step is a separate line on your quote</figcaption>
</figure>

Paid ads from your handle are worth the most to a brand: the ad shows your name and face to people who don’t follow you, and it usually performs like a recommendation, not an ad. That’s why Later’s 2026 benchmarks price it as its own add-on, on top of usage. Google even tells advertisers that running a creator’s video as an ad may need a separate agreement with the creator. That agreement is your fee.

## Build the quote and the clause

Pick where they can use it and for how long. The quote and the clause update together, so what you price is exactly what you sign.

<DeepDive title="Words you’ll hear, in plain English" hint="Whitelisting, dark posts, paid usage, buyout">
- **Whitelisting:** the older industry word for a brand running ads from your handle. On Meta it’s now *partnership ads*, on TikTok *Spark Ads*, on YouTube *partnership ads* through BrandConnect.
- **Dark post:** an ad that runs from your handle but never appears on your profile grid. Revision Legal lists it with whitelisting as paid usage that needs its own paid license.
- **Paid usage:** any use of your content with ad money behind it, from either account.
- **Organic usage:** reposts and placements with no ad spend: their feed, site, emails.
- **Buyout:** selling the rights for a very long time or forever. Price it as its own number, not an add-on.
- **Term starts on:** the go-live date for usage, and the first ad’s start date for paid ads. Write both down so the end date is checkable.
</DeepDive>
