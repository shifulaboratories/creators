<li><strong>Find the rights</strong><span>Usage length, channels, paid ads and exclusivity.</span></li>
<li><strong>Mark changes</strong><span>Propose replacement wording, not just deletions.</span></li>
<li><strong>Decide on a lawyer</strong><span>Big fees, long terms or anything unclear: get a review.</span></li>
