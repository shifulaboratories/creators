**The short version:** the moment a brand or a platform pays you, you're running a small business. Nobody withholds tax for you, so the bill shows up all at once unless you plan for it. Five boring habits fix most of it, and none of them need an accountant on day one.

:::caution[Not tax, legal or financial advice]
This section explains general US federal rules for tax year 2026 so you can ask better questions. Your state, your country and your situation change the answer. Before you file, form a company or make an election, talk to a CPA or enrolled agent.
:::

## Set these up first

<figure class="sc-figure">
<ol class="sc-steps">
<li class="is-active"><strong>Open a separate account</strong><span>One account for creator money in and business costs out. Nothing personal goes through it.</span></li>
<li><strong>Open a tax account</strong><span>A second savings account. Move a slice of every payment into it the day it lands.</span></li>
<li><strong>Know your slice</strong><span>The <a href="/money/set-aside/">set-aside calculator</a> gives you a percentage from your own numbers.</span></li>
<li><strong>Pay four times a year</strong><span>Federal estimated tax is due in April, June, September and January.</span></li>
<li><strong>Keep receipts as you go</strong><span>Fifteen minutes a week beats a lost weekend in April.</span></li>
</ol>
