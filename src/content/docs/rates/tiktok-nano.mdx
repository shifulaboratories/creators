## On TikTok, views beat followers

Later notes that TikTok rates are usually based on your average views, not your follower count. Your For You page reach can be far bigger, or far smaller, than your following. If your last 10 videos average more views than you have followers, quote from views and say so. If a brand wants to run your video as a Spark Ad, price that separately: it’s paid media from your handle.

## At this size

Plenty of brands will offer free product instead of money at this size. Product is fine if you want it, but in the US it counts as income when it’s in exchange for a post (see [taxes](/money/taxes-101/)). Your edge is trust: small audiences often engage more, so lead your pitch with your [engagement rate](/tools/engagement-rate-calculator/), saves and replies.

## Add these on top

- **Usage rights** (the brand reposts your content): +30% to 50%, per Later’s 2026 guide.
- **Paid ads from your handle** (whitelisting, partnership ads, Spark Ads): +30% to 50%, also Later.
- **Peak season** (Q4, Black Friday): Collabstr sees prices rise 20% to 30%.
- **Exclusivity:** no published number. Price it as the deals you’ll turn down.
