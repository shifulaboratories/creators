## Reels, feed posts and Stories

Brands mostly buy Reels now, and Later’s 2026 guide prices Reels above static feed posts at the same size. Stories are usually an add-on: few publishers print a Stories-only price, so bundle them (“1 Reel + 3 Stories”) rather than selling them alone. Instagram also lets a brand run your post as an ad through partnership ads. That’s a separate line on your quote, not a free extra.

## At this size

Brands start asking for usage rights, paid ads from your handle and exclusivity as standard. Each one is worth real money, so never let them ride along for free. This is also where many creators bring in a manager or agent. Check their cut with the [commission calculator](/tools/agency-commission-calculator/) before you sign.

## Add these on top

- **Usage rights** (the brand reposts your content): +30% to 50%, per Later’s 2026 guide.
- **Paid ads from your handle** (whitelisting, partnership ads, Spark Ads): +30% to 50%, also Later.
- **Peak season** (Q4, Black Friday): Collabstr sees prices rise 20% to 30%.
- **Exclusivity:** no published number. Price it as the deals you’ll turn down.
