## How to use these numbers

- **Start your quote near the middle, not the bottom.** Brands counter. Collabstr’s creators listed YouTube work at $311 on average and were paid $255.
- **Price usage, ads and exclusivity separately.** They are where most of the money is once you’re past 100K.
- **Views can matter more than followers,** especially on TikTok and YouTube.

The thinking behind all of this is in [Pricing your rates](/deals/pricing/). For a quote at your exact size, use the [rate calculator](/tools/brand-deal-rate-calculator/).
