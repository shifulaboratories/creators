## Views first, followers second

A brand pays for the people who see the post. That’s why your average views matter more than your follower count, especially on TikTok and Reels, where many views come from people who don’t follow you. If your views are well above what’s typical for your follower count, a views-based quote is your strongest argument for pricing above the published range.

## What this doesn’t include

The base price covers making the post and sharing it on your own account. Anything else is priced separately: the brand reusing your video on its channels, running it as an ad from your handle, keeping you away from competitors, extra revisions, or a rush. See [Pricing your rates](/deals/pricing/) for how to quote, and the [rate calculator](/tools/brand-deal-rate-calculator/) for every published range by size and format.
