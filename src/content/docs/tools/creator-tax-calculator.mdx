**How it works:** choose your filing status, then slide to your creator profit for the year, any day-job pay, and your state’s rate. You’ll see the percentage to set aside from every payment, the split by tax, and roughly what each quarterly payment should be.

<SetAside />

:::caution[Not tax advice]
This is an estimate using US federal rules for 2026. Your state, credits and deductions change the answer. Talk to a CPA or enrolled agent before you file.
:::

## Make it automatic

Open a second savings account just for tax. Every time a brand or platform pays you, move the percentage above into it the same day. Pay from it four times a year. The due dates, the safe harbor rule and how to pay are in [Set aside and pay quarterly](/money/set-aside/).
