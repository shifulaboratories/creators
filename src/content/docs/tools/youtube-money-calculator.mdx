## RPM, not CPM

YouTube Studio shows two numbers that get mixed up. **CPM** is what advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions, before YouTube takes its share. **RPM** is what *you* earned per 1,000 views, after YouTube’s share and across all your revenue sources. RPM is the one that matters for your income, and it’s the one this calculator uses. Find it in YouTube Studio under Analytics, then Revenue.

## Shorts are changing in 2027

YouTube announced that from **February 1, 2027**, channels need 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days to earn ad and subscription revenue on Shorts. Channels below that stay in the Partner Program and keep earning on long videos. Shorts creators who do qualify keep 45% of their allocated Shorts revenue, per YouTube’s Shorts monetization policies.
