**How it works:** pick who it’s for, what you make and your angle. It writes your niche as one line, then runs it past six checks: can you make 50 of these, would a brand pay to reach these people, and so on.

## A niche is a promise, not a cage

Your niche tells a new viewer what they’ll get if they follow. You can widen it once people trust you. Start narrow enough that the right person thinks “this is for me”. Examples and the reasoning are in [Pick your niche](/grow/niche/).
