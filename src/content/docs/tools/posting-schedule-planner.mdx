## Consistency beats volume

Buffer’s study of more than 100,000 accounts over 26 weeks found that people who posted in 20 or more of those weeks got over 5 times the engagement per post of people who posted in 4 weeks or fewer. Pick the pace you can hold for three months, not the one that sounds impressive. The details and the studies are in [Cadence](/grow/cadence/).
