## Read this before you sign with an agency

Chatting agencies often take 30% to 50% or more. That only pays off if they grow sales by more than their cut. The calculator shows the break-even number; ask any agency to show you, in writing, how they’ll beat it. Our [red flags guide](/paywall/agency-red-flags/) lists what to watch for, and the [platform comparison](/paywall/compare/) covers each platform’s terms.
