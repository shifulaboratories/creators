## What to check before you sign anyone

- **Is the commission on gross or net?** 20% of the full fee and 20% of what’s left after other cuts are different numbers.
- **Does it apply to deals they didn’t bring?** Some contracts take a cut of every deal, including ones that came to you directly.
- **What happens after you leave?** A “sunset” clause can keep paying them for months or years on deals they set up.
