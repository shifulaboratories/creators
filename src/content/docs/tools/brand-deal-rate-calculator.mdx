## How to use the number

- **Start near the middle, not the bottom.** Brands counter. On Collabstr’s marketplace, creators listed YouTube work at $311 on average and were paid $255.
- **Price extras as separate lines.** Usage rights and running ads from your handle add 30% to 50% each in Later’s 2026 guide. If they aren’t on the quote, you’re giving them away.
- **On TikTok, lead with views.** Later notes TikTok rates are usually based on average views. If your views beat your follower count, quote on views.

The full reasoning, and three ways to price, are in [Pricing your rates](/deals/pricing/). Then [negotiate](/deals/negotiation/) from there.
