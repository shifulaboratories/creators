**How it works:** pick a look, fill in your numbers, and your kit draws itself as you type. Choose a size: **Page** downloads a PDF to attach to a pitch email, **Story** and **Post** save an image for DMs, Stories and link-in-bio pages. It’s saved on this device as you go. We start you with an example so you can see what good looks like. Tap **Start over** to clear it.

## What brands look for first

1. **Average views, not just followers.** Brands buying TikTok and Reels care what a post actually reaches. Use averages from your last 10 or so posts, from your own insights.
2. **Who your audience is.** Top countries, the main age group and the gender split, copied from your app’s audience insights. A brand selling in the US needs to see US viewers.
3. **Proof it works.** One concrete result beats a wall of logos: sales, sign-ups, sell-outs, saves. If you don’t have one yet, leave it off.
4. **How to reach you.** An email you check. Brands won’t chase.

Work out your engagement rate with the [engagement rate calculator](/tools/engagement-rate-calculator/), and price your packages with the [rate calculator](/tools/brand-deal-rate-calculator/). Our guide to [pitching brands](/deals/pitching-brands/) covers what to send with it.

## Your data stays yours

There’s no account and no database behind this page. Your kit lives in your browser’s storage on this device, and the PDF and images are drawn by your browser, so nothing is uploaded to make them. A share link carries the kit inside the link itself, compressed after the `#`, which browsers never send to a server, so we can’t see it either. Your photo never goes in the link: people who open it see your initial instead. To show your photo, send the PDF or the image.
