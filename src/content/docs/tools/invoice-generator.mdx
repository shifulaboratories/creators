## Get paid faster

- **Ask for the PO or campaign number before you invoice.** Many brands and agencies can’t pay an invoice without one, and it’s the most common reason payments sit.
- **Send it to the billing email, not just your contact.** Your contact approves the work. Accounts payable pays it. Copy both.
- **Name each deliverable the way the contract does.** “1 Instagram Reel, approved by brand, posted Mar 4” matches their records. “Content” doesn’t.
- **Put the terms on the invoice.** Net 30 means payment is due 30 days after the invoice date. If your contract says Net 30, the invoice should too.

Chasing a late payment? [Getting paid](/deals/getting-paid/) has the follow-up emails to send at 1, 15 and 30 days late.
