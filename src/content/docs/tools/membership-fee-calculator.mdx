## Before you pick a platform

- **Fees aren’t the whole story.** A platform with a bigger cut but your audience already on it can earn more than a cheaper one nobody visits.
- **Check who owns the list.** On some platforms you can export your members’ emails. On others, they belong to the app.
- **App store prices are different.** When fans pay inside an iPhone or Android app, Apple or Google can take a cut before the platform does.

The full comparison is in [Paywalls and memberships](/earn/paywalls-and-memberships/).
