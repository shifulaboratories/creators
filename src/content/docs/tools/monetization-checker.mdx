## Platform pay is the smallest check

Ad revenue and creator funds are the money most people think of first, but for most creators they’re not the biggest. Brand deals, fan subscriptions and your own products usually pay more per follower, and none of them has a bar to clear. See [ways creators make money](/earn/monetization-paths/) for how they compare, and the [YouTube money calculator](/tools/youtube-money-calculator/) for what ads pay once you’re in.

## YouTube changes in 2027

YouTube has announced new Partner Program terms from **February 1, 2027**. New channels will need 8,000 watch hours in the last 365 days or 20 million Shorts views in 90 days for ad and Premium revenue, and Shorts revenue sharing will need 10 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days. Channels already in the program aren’t affected. Accept the new terms in YouTube Studio by January 31, 2027.
