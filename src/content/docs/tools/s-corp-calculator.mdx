**How it works:** enter your profit, a reasonable salary for the work you do, and what payroll and an extra tax return would cost you. It compares self-employment tax as a sole proprietor or LLC with payroll tax as an S corp, minus the running costs.

<ScorpCheck />

:::caution[Not tax advice]
An S corp election is hard to undo and has rules on salary that the IRS checks. Run the numbers with a CPA before you file Form 2553.
:::

The full trade-offs, including what an LLC does and doesn’t protect, are in [LLC or S corp?](/money/llc-vs-s-corp/).
