## YouTube cares if people were glad they watched

YouTube’s own explainers list clicks, watch time, survey responses, shares, likes and dislikes as signals, and it surveys viewers to count time on videos they rate highly as more valuable. So engagement per view is useful for a media kit, but watch time and returning viewers tell you more about growth. More in the [YouTube guide](/platforms/youtube/). To turn views into income, try the [YouTube money calculator](/tools/youtube-money-calculator/).

**Where to find your numbers:** YouTube Studio, then **Content**, then a video’s **Analytics**, then **Engagement**. Use the “new subscribers” box for subscribers gained per video.
