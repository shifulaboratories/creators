## On TikTok, use views

Most TikTok views come from the For You feed, from people who don’t follow you yet. That makes engagement by followers swing wildly from video to video. Engagement by views is the steadier number, and Later notes brands usually price TikTok on average views anyway. TikTok says it weighs stronger signals more, such as whether someone watches a longer video to the end. More in the [TikTok guide](/platforms/tiktok/).

**Where to find your numbers:** open a video, tap the three dots, then **Analytics**. Note views, likes, comments, shares and favorites for your last 10 videos and average them.
