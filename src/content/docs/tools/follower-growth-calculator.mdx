## Why milestones still matter

Most feeds now recommend posts from accounts people don’t follow, so a follower count says less about your reach than it used to. But some numbers still unlock things: pay programs on YouTube, TikTok and X have follower or subscriber bars (check yours with the [monetization checker](/tools/monetization-checker/)), and many brands sort creators into tiers by followers before they look at anything else (see [published rates by tier](/tools/brand-deal-rate-calculator/)).

## Growing faster

The levers that move the pace are mostly about each post, not about posting more: the first seconds ([Hooks that hold](/grow/hooks/)), a clear niche ([Pick your niche](/grow/niche/)), and reading what your analytics tell you ([Read your analytics](/grow/analytics/)). Change one thing at a time, and check this calculator again in a month.
