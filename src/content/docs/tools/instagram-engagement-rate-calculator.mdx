## Likes are not the number Instagram watches

Instagram’s head has named **sends** (people sharing your post in DMs) as a top signal in every statement we cite from 2024 and 2025. So a Reel with modest likes but lots of sends is often doing better than it looks. Your engagement rate is still the number brands ask for, so know it, but judge your own Reels by sends per reach. More in the [Instagram guide](/platforms/instagram/).

**Where to find your numbers:** open a post or Reel, tap **View insights**, and note likes, comments, shares, saves and views. Do it for your last 10 posts and average them.
