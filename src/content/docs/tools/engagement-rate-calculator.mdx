## Which number to show a brand

- **By followers** is the classic number and what most brands mean when they ask. It falls as you grow, which is normal.
- **By views** is fairer on TikTok, Reels and Shorts, where most views come from people who don’t follow you yet. Show it when your views are higher than your follower count.
- **Saves and shares count most.** Platforms say they look at shares and saves as strong signals, so a post with fewer likes but lots of sends is often doing better than it looks. See [Read your analytics](/grow/analytics/).

There is no official benchmark from any platform. Later’s 2026 pricing guide treats 1% to 5% as typical, and you’ll see other ranges elsewhere. Compare yourself with your own last month first.
