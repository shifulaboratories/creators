**The short version:** if you made it, you own the copyright, and every platform has a free takedown form. File it yourself, one link per item, with a link to your original. For intimate images shared without consent, use StopNCII.org (adults) or Take It Down (under 18) first: they stop the image spreading across many platforms at once.

## Intimate images: do this first

- **StopNCII.org** creates a digital fingerprint (a hash) of the image on your own device; the image itself isn't uploaded. Participating platforms, including Meta's, use the hash to block it.
- **Take It Down** from NCMEC does the same for images taken when you were under 18. Its site lists the participating platforms.
- Also report the post on the platform itself, and keep evidence. In many places this is a crime: you can go to the police.

## File a takedown on each platform

| Platform | Where to file | Who can file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **YouTube** | [Copyright removal request](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2807622) (also in Studio for eligible channels) | The copyright owner or their agent |
| **Instagram** | [Copyright report form](https://help.instagram.com/contact/552695131608132/) | The owner or their agent |
| **TikTok** | Linked from its [Intellectual Property Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/copyright-policy/en) | The owner or their agent |
| **X** | Linked from its [Copyright policy](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/copyright-policy) | The owner or their agent |
| **Google Search** | Google's [legal removal requests](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/3110420) | The owner or their agent |

**What every form asks for:** your contact details, a link to your original, the exact link to each copy, and a statement that you believe the use isn't authorized. Filing one is a legal statement, so only report your own work.

## If paid content leaks

If your paid or subscriber-only content is reposted on piracy sites, the same DMCA route applies: file with the site's host, and with Google to drop the page from search results. Paid takedown services exist; check our [Stack](/stack/) for tools listed with free alternatives before you pay.
