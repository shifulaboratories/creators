**The short version:** recover the login first (your email or Google Account), then kick out every device you don't know, then clean up what they posted. Do it today: some platforms only help with recent incidents.

## Recover it on your platform

<Playbook situation="hacked" />

## If they also have your email

Every "forgot password" link goes to your email, so an attacker who owns it can keep taking the account back. Recover the email account first, using your email provider's own recovery flow, and change its password before anything else. Then work through the platform steps above.

## Spot it happening right now

- An email saying your login email, phone or password changed, and you didn't do it.
- Posts, stories, DMs or livestreams you didn't make. TikTok lists deleted videos and changed usernames as common signs.
- Your phone suddenly has no signal. That can be a [SIM swap](/protect/scams/), which hands your texted codes to someone else.

## Don't do this

- **Don't pay "recovery experts".** People who comment "I can get your account back" under posts about hacks are almost always scammers. The platforms' own routes are free.
- **Don't make a new account to post "I was hacked" and then abandon the old one.** Recover the original: it has your audience, history and monetization.
- **Don't wait.** YouTube only supports hacked-channel cases from the past 9 months, and a hacker's posts can earn strikes on your account while it's out of your hands.

## Once you're back in

Run the [15-minute lockdown checklist](/protect/account-security/). Most takeovers start with a password reused somewhere else, a phishing link, or a texted code.
