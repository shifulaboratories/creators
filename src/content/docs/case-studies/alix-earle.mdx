**The short version:** Alix Earle didn't invent a new format. She took Get Ready With Me, one of the most crowded formats on TikTok, and used the makeup as the backdrop while she told unfiltered stories about her life. People came back for her, not the products.

Her follower count nearly tripled in about a month, according to figures Glossy reported from an influencer marketer who tracked her. Her first TikTok went up in February 2020, so this was not an overnight start. It was two and a half years of posting, then a breakout.

## The move: the format is a container

Glossy described her makeup as "visual distractions" while she talks, often about the night before. That is the trick: a familiar format gets the first second of attention, and the story keeps people to the end.

## What came after

Brands followed the audience. Glossy reported deals with Grubhub and White Fox Boutique and a Tarte brand trip by early 2023, with one marketer estimating at least $40,000 per sponsored post at the time. That's an estimate, not a figure she published. She made Forbes 30 Under 30 in 2023 and TIME100 Creators in 2025.

Not every next step held. In 2025, NBC News reported that Hot Mess was dropped from Unwell after a reported fallout. Owning your audience is different from owning the show you build on it. See [Alex Cooper's story](/case-studies/alex-cooper/) for the other side of that lesson.

## What to take from it

1. **Pick a format people already watch, then make it yours.** You don't need a new idea. You need a new reason to stay. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
2. **The hook and the payoff can be different things.** The makeup gets the tap, the story earns the follow. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
3. **Honesty is a format choice.** Talking about acne and bad nights made her relatable in a feed full of polish. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
4. **Breakouts sit on top of a long runway.** Two and a half years of posting came before the month that changed everything. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
