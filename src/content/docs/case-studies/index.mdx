## The one move each of them made

<figure class="sc-figure">
<ol class="sc-steps">
<li class="is-active"><strong>MrBeast</strong><span>Designs the thumbnail before the video and obsesses over the first minute.</span></li>
<li><strong>Ryan Trahan</strong><span>Turned vlogs into seasons with a goal the audience could help reach.</span></li>
<li><strong>Alix Earle</strong><span>Used a familiar format as the backdrop for stories only she could tell.</span></li>
<li><strong>Ali Abdaal</strong><span>Built five-plus income streams so ads became the smaller half.</span></li>
<li><strong>Alex Cooper</strong><span>Took ownership of her show, then licensed it for nine figures.</span></li>
<li><strong>Khaby Lame</strong><span>Made videos with no words, so they worked in every country.</span></li>
<li><strong>Tabitha Brown</strong><span>Took the same warm voice from Facebook to TikTok, then kept ownership of what it built.</span></li>
<li><strong>Keith Lee</strong><span>Pays for every meal, so viewers believe his scores.</span></li>
<li><strong>Kayla Itsines</strong><span>Let her clients' results do the marketing, then moved them into an app she owned.</span></li>
<li><strong>Emma Chamberlain</strong><span>Changed formats before a 30-hour edit schedule burned her out.</span></li>
<li><strong>Jackie Aina</strong><span>Served the audience beauty brands ignored, and turned that trust into leverage.</span></li>
<li><strong>Vivian Tu</strong><span>Put a Wall Street résumé in a best friend's voice.</span></li>
<li><strong>Steven Bartlett</strong><span>Gave failure a job title and tested almost everything.</span></li>
