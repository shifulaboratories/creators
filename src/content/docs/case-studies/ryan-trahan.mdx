## The move: a series with a scoreboard

In 2022 he started with one penny and tried to trade and work his way to meeting MrBeast in 30 days, posting a vlog every day. The charity goal was $100,000 for Feeding America. Tubefilter reported the series passed 192 million views and raised more than $1.3 million, and he gained 2 million subscribers over the month.

Each time he ran the idea again, the goal got bigger and so did the result.

## How the format works

For 50 States in 50 Days, Trahan and his wife Haley Pham posted one video a day while road-tripping for St. Jude. They hit the original $1 million goal within two weeks, according to YouTube's own blog. A "Wheel of Doom" added penalties, and a $50,000 donation spun it, so donors could change the story.

## What to take from it

1. **Package your uploads as a season.** A set number of episodes with a finish line turns a casual viewer into someone following a story. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
2. **Give the audience a lever.** Votes, donations or comments that change what happens next make people come back to see their effect. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
3. **Rerun what worked, bigger.** He didn't invent a new idea each year. He raised the stakes on the same one. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
4. **A cause can be the hook.** Charity gave the series a goal everyone could root for, and brands joined on their own, per YouTube's blog. <span class="sc-tag is-platform">From the platform</span>
