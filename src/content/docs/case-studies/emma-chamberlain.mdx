**The short version:** Emma Chamberlain grew on a vlog style that was hard to copy because it was so much work. Instead of grinding that format until she broke, she changed formats: a talk podcast that needs no 30-hour edit, a coffee brand built from a habit she already showed on camera, and fewer, longer videos.

## The move: a signature style, priced in hours

In 2019 she told Tubefilter she spent 20 to 30 hours editing each vlog, down from 30 to 40 when she started, with no assistant editors. "I had never seen anyone edit the way that I edit before I did it, and it's just what felt right to me." That made her videos feel different from everything around them. It also made them hard to sustain: "So much work goes into each video that I don't know how I'm still alive."

In August 2021 she announced a break of several weeks from YouTube. "I'm thinking a lot about how I can evolve and make things that truly excite me," she posted, as quoted by Tubefilter. She came back posting less often, and leaned on formats that suited her better.

## What came after

The podcast was the business. "The key to turning something into a business is making it consistent, and I know I can be consistent with the podcast," she told Fast Company in 2023. Chamberlain Coffee raised a $7 million Series A in 2022 (Fortune). Forbes estimated its 2023 revenue at about $20 million, nearly double 2022. That's Forbes's estimate, not a company figure.

A consumer brand brings its own risks. In March 2025 The Ankler reported that the coffee company's growth slowed sharply in 2024 and that it struggled to raise money amid executive turnover. She's said she is "rebuilding my relationship with YouTube and trying to find a way to make it healthy and fun" (Forbes Australia, 2024).

## What to take from it

1. **Price your format in hours before you commit to a schedule.** A style that takes 30 hours a video can't run weekly forever without a team. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
2. **You can pause in public.** She said why she was stepping back, and came back with fewer, different videos. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
3. **Move your personality to a cheaper medium.** A talk podcast kept her weekly with far less editing, and a platform paid to license it. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
4. **Build products from what you already show.** Her coffee habit was on camera before it was a company. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
