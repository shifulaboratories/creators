Vivian Tu combined two things that rarely go together: the credibility of a former JPMorgan trader and the voice of a friend who just wants you to stop getting ripped off. She aimed it at people finance media had overlooked, then protected that trust in every deal.

## The move: credential plus persona

Tu started as an equities trader at JPMorgan, then moved to BuzzFeed (Fortune). She posted her first TikTok on January 1, 2021, and it took off the same day. "I want to tell you that I had this evil mastermind plan to build this all out, but I didn't," she told Fortune. What she did have was a clear pitch: "It's like, here are the rules of the game. I'm going to teach you how to play."

Her mission, as Business Insider put it, is making finance less "male, pale, and stale." The persona does the rest. "Even if you can't have those conversations with your own friends, you can have them with mine," she told Business Insider.

## What came after

Brands came to her, and she's choosy about which ones. "It's so hard to build trust and so easy to lose it," she told Retail TouchPoints in 2024, and "it's not about who's going to give me the most money for one video." Her deals bundle time on set, content for her own channels, exclusivity and usage rights, and a bonus if the content outperforms. Early partners were banks and brokerages, and later consumer brands followed (Retail TouchPoints).

Each format fed the next. When she added video to the podcast, she said, "I wanted to work smarter so I didn't have to duplicate work" (Business Insider). She frames her content as education rather than personal advice: "I'm not going to know whether a Roth IRA makes sense for everyone" (Fortune). That matters in a category regulators watch closely.

## What to take from it

1. **Combine a hard credential with a soft voice.** Either one alone is common. Together they're rare. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
2. **Name the audience your category ignores.** Nobody else was talking to them, so they showed up fast. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
3. **Price trust, not posts.** Long-term partners, owned content and performance bonuses beat the biggest one-off check. See [Brand deals](/deals/). <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
