**The short version:** Khaby Lame reacts to overcomplicated "life hack" videos by doing the task the obvious way, in silence, then finishing with the same open-palm gesture. No words means no language barrier, and that let one Italian creator reach the whole world.

## How big it got

In about two years he went from a factory layoff to passing Charli D'Amelio as TikTok's most-followed creator. CNN and The Verge both reported the milestone on June 23, 2022. His manager told Forbes and Fortune in 2022 that he earned up to $750,000 per post. That figure comes from his team, not from published accounts.

## What to take from it

1. **Design for the sound-off, worldwide viewer.** If your video works without words or captions, every market is your market. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
2. **React to what's already trending.** Stitching a viral video borrows its momentum. Add a twist that makes yours the one people share. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
3. **Repeat a signature beat.** A recurring ending makes a scroller recognize you before they read your name. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
