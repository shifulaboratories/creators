**The short version:** Ali Abdaal makes videos about productivity and has published his income on camera, stream by stream, year by year. Two things stand out: YouTube ad revenue was tiny for the first three years, and by 2022 it was less than half of what he earned from sponsors, affiliates, podcasts and his newsletter combined.

All numbers below are what he said in his own videos. He rounds some and calls them "ish," and he says the totals weren't fully reconciled with his accountant, so treat them as close, not audited.

Subscribers followed the same curve. He reported 1,600 at the end of 2017, 125,000 in 2018, 487,000 in 2019, 1.32 million in 2020, 2.5 million in 2021 and 3.6 million in 2022. Growth looks slow, then sudden. That's what compounding looks like from the inside.

## What to take from it

1. **Expect the first years to pay almost nothing.** His ad income was under $50,000 a year until his third full year. Plan around that. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
2. **Build more than one income stream early.** His advice in the video: "you might as well sign up for Amazon Associates from day one." <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
3. **Sponsors pay for trust, so protect it.** Only promoting what you use keeps the audience that sponsors are paying to reach. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
4. **Niche changes the math.** He says he'd pick finance or business if starting over, because those audiences pay more per view. <span class="sc-tag is-practitioner">Creator-tested</span>
