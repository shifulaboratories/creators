**The short version:** we explain how things work so you can make better decisions and ask better questions. We're not your lawyer, accountant or financial adviser, and reading this site doesn't make us one.

## Not professional advice

Everything on Select Creators is general educational information. It isn't legal, tax, financial, investment or accounting advice, and it isn't tailored to you.

- **Contracts and deals.** Our contract and negotiation guides explain what clauses usually mean and what to ask about. Before you sign something that matters, have a lawyer who represents you read it. Laws differ by country and state, and one clause can change the meaning of another.
- **Money and taxes.** Earnings, pricing and business-setup pages describe common approaches. Tax rules depend on where you live and how you're set up. Talk to an accountant or tax professional.
- **No client relationship.** Reading the site, contributing to it, or messaging us doesn't create an attorney-client, adviser-client or agency relationship with anyone.

## Platforms change constantly

Algorithms, monetization programs, payout rates and rules change, often without notice. Every guide shows when it was last reviewed. Something that was accurate on that date may not be true today, so check the platform's own help center before you rely on a rule or a number.

When we describe how an algorithm works, we're reporting what platforms have published and what their people have said on the record. Nobody outside a platform can see the whole system. We label guesses as guesses.

## No guaranteed results

Case studies and examples show what happened for specific creators at a specific time. Your results will be different. Growth depends on your niche, your content, your timing and a lot of luck. Anyone who guarantees you a result is selling something.

## We're not affiliated with the platforms

Select Creators is independent. We're not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube, Google, TikTok, ByteDance, Instagram, Meta, X, Spotify or any other company we write about. Their names and trademarks belong to them, and we use them only to identify the products we're discussing.

## The Underground section is journalism

Our Underground pages describe gray-market practices, like bought followers, paid verification and handle theft, so creators can recognize and avoid them. Describing a practice isn't endorsing it. Many of these practices break platform rules, and some break the law. We don't link to sellers, and nothing there is meant as instructions. Statements about people and companies come from published reporting or court records, and allegations are described as allegations.

## Links to other sites

We link to sources, tools and platforms we don't control. We're not responsible for their content, prices, privacy practices or availability. Prices and features change, so check before you buy. Affiliate links are labeled where they appear. See [How we make money](/about/how-we-make-money/).

## AI answers

Every page has buttons to send it to AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT and Perplexity. Those answers are generated by the AI company, not by us, and they can be wrong or outdated. Check anything important against the sources on the page.

## Community contributions

This is an open-source project and many people contribute. Pages are reviewed against our [editorial standards](/about/editorial-standards/) before they're published, but a contributor's views are their own until a page is marked **Fact-checked**.

## Spotted a problem?

If something is wrong, [tell us](/about/corrections/). We'd rather fix it than defend it.
