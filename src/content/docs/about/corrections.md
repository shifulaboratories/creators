**The short version:** if something here is wrong, we want to know. Tell us, we check it against the sources, and if we got it wrong we fix it in public with a dated note. We don't quietly edit mistakes away.

## How to reach us

| What you want to tell us | Best way |
| --- | --- |
| A fact is wrong or out of date | [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/shifulaboratories/creators/issues/new), or use **Edit page** at the bottom of any page and propose the fix yourself |
| A link is broken or an affiliate link isn't labeled | [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/shifulaboratories/creators/issues/new) |
| You're named on a page and something about you is wrong | Message us privately on [Instagram (@selectcreators)](https://www.instagram.com/selectcreators/). GitHub issues are public, so don't post personal details there |
| Your copyrighted work is used without permission | Message us privately on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/selectcreators/) with a link to your original and the page that uses it |
| Safety: a page exposes private information or puts someone at risk | Message us privately on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/selectcreators/) and start the message with **Safety** |

Tell us which page, what's wrong, and if you can, a source that shows the right answer.

## What a correction looks like

When we fix a factual error, the page gets a note at the bottom like this:

> **Correction, [date]:** An earlier version of this page gave the wrong year for a platform's policy change. The change took effect in 2024, not 2023. We've updated the section.

That one's an example. Real notes name the actual mistake, plainly.

Small things like typos, formatting and broken links are fixed without a note. Every change to every page, big or small, is also visible in the page's history on GitHub, so nothing disappears without a trace.

## If you're named on a page

We take this seriously. Here's how we handle it:

- **Something about you is factually wrong:** we correct it, with a note, as fast as we can verify it.
- **You weren't asked for comment:** we'll add your response or a link to your statement if you send one.
- **The page exposes private details:** home address, phone number, family members, anything that could get you hurt. We remove it first and review later.
- **The case was dropped, settled or decided in your favor:** send us the record and we'll update the page to say so.

What we won't do is remove accurate, sourced reporting just because it's unflattering. The Underground section exists to help creators protect themselves, and that only works if the record stays intact. If you disagree with how a story is told, we'll still publish your side.

## Copyright

We link to and briefly quote other people's work to report on it and credit it. We don't host leaked documents or rehost other people's videos. If you believe a page uses your work beyond that, message us with your original and the page in question, and we'll review it and remove anything that shouldn't be there.
