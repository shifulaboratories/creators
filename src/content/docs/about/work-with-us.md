**The short version:** Select Creators is built by the team behind a new, tech-forward talent agency for creators. The handbook is free and complete on its own. If you'd like hands-on help with brand deals, contracts or building your team, you can reach us directly.

## What the agency does

Deals, contracts, negotiation and team-building for creators, with the same standards you see on this site: clear terms, sourced advice, and no surprises about who gets paid what.

## How to reach us

Message us on [Instagram (@selectcreators)](https://www.instagram.com/selectcreators/) and tell us a little about what you make and what you need help with.

## How this affects the handbook

It doesn't change what we publish. Nothing in the guides is held back to make you hire us, competitors get fair coverage, and if a page mentions someone the agency works with, it says so. The full rules are in [How we make money](/about/how-we-make-money/).
