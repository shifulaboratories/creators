**The short version:** this handbook is only as good as the people who check it. If you know something we got wrong, or something we haven't covered, you can fix it yourself in a few minutes. No coding needed for most changes.

### Flag a problem

Spotted an outdated stat, a broken link or a claim that doesn't match its source? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/shifulaboratories/creators/issues/new) and tell us the page and what's wrong. That's it. A free GitHub account is all you need.

### Fix it yourself

Every page has an **Edit page** link at the bottom. It opens the page on GitHub, where you can edit the text right in your browser.

<figure class="sc-figure">
<ol class="sc-steps">
<li class="is-active"><strong>Tap Edit page</strong><span>At the bottom of any guide. GitHub asks you to sign in and makes your own copy automatically.</span></li>
<li><strong>Make the change</strong><span>Pages are plain text with a little formatting. Add a link to your source.</span></li>
<li><strong>Propose it</strong><span>GitHub calls this a pull request. Say what you changed and why.</span></li>

### Add a tool

The Stack section is built from small files in [`src/data/tools/`](https://github.com/shifulaboratories/creators/tree/main/src/data/tools), one per tool. Copy an existing one and fill it in. You'll need to name a free or non-affiliate alternative: the site won't build without one.

### Write a guide

Pages marked **Coming soon** are outlines waiting for a writer. Pick one, [open an issue](https://github.com/shifulaboratories/creators/issues/new) saying you'd like to take it, and an editor will help you shape the angle and sources before you start writing. That saves you from writing something we'd have to send back.

## What we're looking for

- **Creators** who can say "here's what actually happened when I tried this," with numbers.
- **Managers, agents and lawyers** who can explain what's normal in a deal or contract.
- **People who work, or worked, at platforms** and can point to public sources we've missed.
- **Fact-checkers.** Honestly, the most valuable thing anyone can do is open our sources and check we got them right.

## The rules, in short

1. **Link your source.** A platform statement, published reporting, code, or say clearly that it's your first-hand experience.
2. **Label how sure you are.** Sourced, creator-tested or unconfirmed. See [Editorial standards](/about/editorial-standards/).
3. **Declare conflicts.** If you're paid by, invest in, or work for something you're writing about, say so in your pull request.
4. **No hidden affiliate links.** Affiliate links only go in tool files marked as affiliate, where the site labels them.
5. **No private individuals.** Don't name people who aren't public figures, and never post anyone's personal details.
6. **Underground pages have extra rules.** No links or handles that help anyone reach a seller, no how-to steps, and every claim cites published reporting or a court record. Read the [Underground overview](/underground/) first.

The full version, including page formatting, lives in the [contribution guide](https://github.com/shifulaboratories/creators/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Credit

Contributors are credited on the pages they write or review, and every change is permanently tied to your GitHub profile in the page history. If you'd rather not be named on the page, say so in your pull request.

## Be decent

Be kind, be specific, and assume good faith. Harassment, doxxing or naming private individuals gets you removed from the project.
