## Who it's for

Anyone making things on the internet and trying to turn that into a living. Beauty, fitness, gaming, finance, comedy, cooking, vlogging: the platforms work the same way underneath, and so do the contracts. If you've ever wondered whether a "post at 7pm" rule is real, whether a usage clause is normal, or what a manager should actually cost, this is for you.

You don't need to be technical. When we explain how an algorithm works, we start with what it means for your next post and save the math for people who want it.

- **Sourced, with the receipts showing.** We rank evidence: published code beats a platform's own explainer, which beats a platform employee on the record, which beats a named creator's experience. Anonymous claims don't make the cut. See [Editorial standards](/about/editorial-standards/).
- **Open source.** Every page lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/shifulaboratories/creators). If something is wrong or out of date, anyone can propose a fix, and contributors get credit on the page. See [Contribute](/about/contribute/).
- **Honest about money.** We tell you exactly how this site earns, and the business behind it. See [How we make money](/about/how-we-make-money/).
- **Built to be read by you and your AI.** Every page has a plain-text version, and a button to hand it to Claude, ChatGPT or Perplexity so you can ask follow-up questions about your own situation.

## Who's behind it

Select Creators is built by the team behind a new, tech-forward talent agency for creators, founded by William Lee. That's a business interest, and we'd rather you hear it from us on day one. The handbook is complete on its own: nothing here is held back to make you hire anyone. [Work with us](/about/work-with-us/) explains how to reach the team if you want hands-on help.

## The promises

1. **Free.** No paywall, no email gate, no "unlock the rest."
2. **Sourced.** If we can't back it up, we say it's a guess or we leave it out.
3. **Fixable.** Errors get corrected in public, with a dated note. See [Corrections and takedowns](/about/corrections/).
4. **Disclosed.** Every affiliate link and sponsor is labeled where it appears.
5. **Safe.** We cover the gray market as journalism so you can protect yourself. We never link to sellers or help anyone use them.
