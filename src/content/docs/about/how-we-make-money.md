Select Creators is free. These are the ways it makes money, and the rules that keep the advice honest.

## Affiliate links

Some tool links are affiliate links: if you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

- Every affiliate link is labeled **affiliate** where it appears, not just in a footer.
- Every recommendation lists a free or non-affiliate alternative.
- Rankings never depend on what a company pays us.
- The Underground section never carries affiliate links, ads or sponsors.

### Affiliate programs we belong to

None yet. Every program we join will be listed here, and changes to this list go through a public pull request.

## Sponsorships

Sponsored reviews and newsletter sponsorships are marked **Sponsored**. Sponsors get no editing rights, and negative findings stay in.

## Who is behind this site

The people who run Select Creators are building a talent agency for creators. We say so up front, so you can weigh our advice with that in mind. The guides here are complete and free whether or not you ever work with us.
