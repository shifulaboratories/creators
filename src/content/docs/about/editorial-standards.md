- **Named authors and reviewers.** Every page shows who wrote it, who reviewed it and when it was last checked.
- **Show your work.** Claims cite a source, a platform statement or a labeled first-hand case.
- **Confidence labels.** `verified` (cited, or first-hand with evidence), `practitioner-view` (expert opinion), `speculative` (theories, especially about algorithms).
- **Freshness.** Algorithm and tool pages older than six months are flagged for review.
- **Case studies.** Only with the creator's written consent or from public information, with numbers the creator approved.
- **Conflicts.** Contributors declare any paid relationship with a product they write about, in the pull request.
- **Not legal or financial advice.** Contract and money pages are educational. For decisions that matter, talk to a professional.
