## What a name on a page means

- **By** means that person wrote the guide, or rewrote most of it.
- **Reviewed by** means a named person read the whole page, checked the sources, and agreed to put their name on it. A page only shows **Fact-checked** once someone has reviewed it this way.
- **No label** means nobody has signed off on it yet. The sources are still there, so you can check them yourself.

Nobody pays to be listed here, and a name is never added to a page someone didn't read.

## Want to be on this page?

We're looking for people who do the work: creators, managers, agents, lawyers, editors and accountants who will put their name on what they write. Start with [how to contribute](/about/contribute/).
