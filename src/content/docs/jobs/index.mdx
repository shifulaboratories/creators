## Before you apply

- **Check the company first.** Look up any agency or management firm in the [company directory](/team/agency-directory/) before you take a call.
- **Know the going rate.** [Hiring your team](/team/hiring/) has what editors and thumbnail designers are paid, from Upwork, Indeed and the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.
- **Get it in writing.** Freelance work should have a short contract that says what you deliver, when you get paid and who owns the files.
