**The short version:** most creator-economy jobs are posted in a few places. Channels hire editors and designers on YT Jobs and freelance marketplaces, companies post on their own careers sites and on creator-economy job boards, and branded shoots hire crew on production boards. Nobody pays to be listed here, and none of these links earn us anything.

<JobBoards />

## Before you apply

- **Check the company first.** Look up any agency or management firm in the [company directory](/team/agency-directory/) before you take a call.
- **Know the going rate.** [Hiring your team](/team/hiring/) has what editors and thumbnail designers are paid, from Upwork, Indeed and the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.
- **Get it in writing.** Freelance work should have a short contract that says what you deliver, when you get paid and who owns the files.

## Hiring instead?

The same boards work the other way. [Hiring your team](/team/hiring/) covers where to post, what to pay and how to try someone out before you commit.
